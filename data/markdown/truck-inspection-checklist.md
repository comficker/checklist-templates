# Truck Inspection Checklist

A DOT-style pre-trip inspection for commercial trucks covering tires, brakes, lights, coupling, fluids and load securement.

> Free printable & editable version: [Truck Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/truck-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Engine Compartment & Fluids

- [ ] Check engine oil level and condition
- [ ] Check coolant level and inspect the radiator
- [ ] Check power steering and washer fluid
- [ ] Inspect belts and hoses for cracks or wear
- [ ] Look for leaks under the engine and components
- [ ] Check the battery is secure with clean connections

## Tires & Wheels

- [ ] Check tire pressure and inflate to spec
- [ ] Measure tread depth against the legal minimum
- [ ] Inspect for cuts, bulges, uneven wear or debris
- [ ] Check for missing or loose lug nuts and rust streaks
- [ ] Inspect rims for cracks or damage
- [ ] Confirm dual tires are not touching and valve caps are present

## Brakes & Air System

- [ ] Perform an air brake leak-down and build-up test
- [ ] Check the low-air warning and emergency brake function
- [ ] Inspect brake chambers, slack adjusters and linings
- [ ] Check brake drums or rotors for cracks or wear
- [ ] Inspect air lines and connections for leaks or damage
- [ ] Drain air tanks of moisture as required

## Lights & Signals

- [ ] Test headlights on low and high beam
- [ ] Check brake lights, tail lights and turn signals
- [ ] Confirm clearance, marker and reflector lights work
- [ ] Test the four-way hazard flashers
- [ ] Check the horn and backup alarm
- [ ] Clean lenses and reflectors for visibility

## Coupling & Trailer

- [ ] Confirm the fifth wheel is locked with no gap — _Check the locking jaws are closed around the kingpin_
- [ ] Inspect the kingpin and apron for damage
- [ ] Check that the release arm is in the locked position
- [ ] Connect and inspect air lines and the electrical cord
- [ ] Check landing gear is fully raised and secured
- [ ] Inspect the trailer frame, doors and mud flaps

## Cab & Load Securement

- [ ] Test steering play, mirrors and the windshield
- [ ] Check seat belt, gauges and warning lights
- [ ] Confirm the fire extinguisher and emergency triangles are present
- [ ] Verify the load is secured, balanced and within weight limits
- [ ] Inspect straps, chains, tarps and tie-downs
- [ ] Confirm required permits and paperwork are aboard
