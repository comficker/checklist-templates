# Beach Vacation Checklist

A complete beach vacation checklist covering sun protection, swimwear, beach gear, the day bag and travel essentials.

> Free printable & editable version: [Beach Vacation Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/beach-vacation-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Sun Protection

- [ ] Broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher — _Check the expiry date and replace anything older than a year._
- [ ] Water-resistant sunscreen for swimming
- [ ] Lip balm with SPF
- [ ] Wide-brimmed hat or cap
- [ ] Sunglasses with UV protection
- [ ] Aloe vera or after-sun lotion
- [ ] Lightweight cover-up or rash guard

## Swimwear and Beachwear

- [ ] Two swimsuits so one can dry
- [ ] Quick-dry shorts and tops
- [ ] Sundresses or light, breathable clothing
- [ ] Flip-flops or water sandals
- [ ] An outfit for evenings out
- [ ] Sleepwear and underwear for the trip length

## Beach Gear

- [ ] Beach towels for each person
- [ ] Beach umbrella or pop-up sun shade
- [ ] Beach chairs or a mat
- [ ] Cooler or insulated bag for drinks
- [ ] Snorkel set, goggles, or floats
- [ ] Beach toys for kids: bucket, shovel, ball
- [ ] Waterproof phone pouch and a small dry bag

## Day Bag for the Shore

- [ ] Reusable water bottles
- [ ] Snacks and fresh fruit
- [ ] Sunscreen for reapplying
- [ ] Wet wipes and hand sanitizer
- [ ] Plastic bag for wet swimsuits
- [ ] Cash and ID in a waterproof pouch
- [ ] A book, magazine, or downloaded entertainment

## Toiletries and Health

- [ ] Travel-sized shampoo, conditioner, and body wash
- [ ] Toothbrush and toothpaste
- [ ] Daily medications and a basic first-aid kit
- [ ] Insect repellent
- [ ] Motion-sickness remedy if you plan boat trips
- [ ] Hairbrush and detangler for salt water

## Travel Essentials

- [ ] ID, passport, and booking confirmations
- [ ] Debit or credit card and cash
- [ ] Phone, charger, and power bank
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] Reusable shopping bag
- [ ] Check the local weather and tide times before each beach day
