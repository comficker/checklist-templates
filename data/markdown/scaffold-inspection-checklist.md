# Scaffold Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting scaffolds before each shift.

> Free printable & editable version: [Scaffold Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/scaffold-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Foundation and base

- [ ] Confirm the ground is firm, level and capable of supporting the load.
- [ ] Check base plates and mud sills are in place and seated.
- [ ] Verify screw jacks are not over-extended beyond their limit.
- [ ] Confirm the scaffold is plumb and level. — _A competent person must judge whether the base and footing are adequate per OSHA 1926 subpart L._
- [ ] Check casters are locked on mobile scaffolds.
- [ ] Verify nothing has undermined or eroded the footing.

## Frames and bracing

- [ ] Inspect frames and standards for bends, cracks or corrosion.
- [ ] Confirm cross bracing is fitted and pinned correctly.
- [ ] Check couplers and connections are tight and undamaged.
- [ ] Verify locking pins and clips are engaged at each level.
- [ ] Confirm components are compatible and not mixed improperly.
- [ ] Check the scaffold has not been altered without authorization.

## Platforms and planking

- [ ] Confirm platforms are fully planked with no gaps over the limit.
- [ ] Inspect planks for cracks, splits, warping or rot.
- [ ] Verify planks are scaffold-grade and rated for the load.
- [ ] Check planks overlap or are cleated to prevent movement.
- [ ] Confirm platforms are clear of debris, ice and tripping hazards.
- [ ] Verify the platform is not overloaded with materials.

## Guardrails and fall protection

- [ ] Confirm top rails are fitted at the required height.
- [ ] Check mid-rails are in place between top rail and platform.
- [ ] Verify toeboards are fitted to prevent falling objects.
- [ ] Confirm fall protection is provided above OSHA trigger heights.
- [ ] Check anchor points for personal fall arrest where used.
- [ ] Verify openings and gaps are guarded or covered.

## Access and clearances

- [ ] Confirm safe access such as a ladder or stair tower is provided.
- [ ] Check that workers do not climb cross braces for access.
- [ ] Verify access points are clear and secured.
- [ ] Confirm safe clearance from power lines is maintained.
- [ ] Check overhead protection where work is above.
- [ ] Verify the area below is barricaded where needed.

## Ties, stability and records

- [ ] Confirm the scaffold is tied to the structure at required intervals.
- [ ] Check the height-to-base ratio is within safe limits or stabilized.
- [ ] Verify outriggers or stabilizers are fitted where required.
- [ ] Confirm weather and wind conditions are safe for use.
- [ ] Tag the scaffold green, yellow or red per its condition.
- [ ] Record the inspection and competent person, then sign and date.
