# Unpacking Checklist

A room-by-room unpacking checklist for the first days in a new home, starting with essentials and finishing with box breakdown and admin.

> Free printable & editable version: [Unpacking Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/unpacking-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Arrival and Safety

- [ ] Walk through the home and check for damage before unpacking
- [ ] Confirm electricity, water, heating and internet are working
- [ ] Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
- [ ] Find the main water shutoff and the electrical panel
- [ ] Change or rekey the locks if possible — _Previous owners, tenants or contractors may still have keys._
- [ ] Check every box and furniture piece arrived against your inventory

## First Night

- [ ] Unpack the first-night box: chargers, medication, toiletries and a change of clothes
- [ ] Assemble and make the beds
- [ ] Put up curtains or temporary covers in bedrooms
- [ ] Set out towels, toilet paper, soap and a shower curtain
- [ ] Keep snacks, water and basic tools within reach

## Kitchen

- [ ] Wipe out cabinets, drawers and the fridge before filling them
- [ ] Unpack plates, cups, cutlery and one pot and pan first
- [ ] Set up the kettle, coffee maker and other daily appliances
- [ ] Store everyday items near where you use them
- [ ] Put cleaning products out of reach of children and pets

## Bathrooms

- [ ] Clean the bathroom before unpacking
- [ ] Unpack toiletries, towels and the medicine cabinet
- [ ] Keep medications stored safely and out of reach

## Bedrooms

- [ ] Unpack clothes for the next week first
- [ ] Hang clothes and fill drawers
- [ ] Set up the children's rooms early to help them settle

## Living Areas and Extras

- [ ] Place large furniture before unpacking smaller items
- [ ] Set up the TV, Wi-Fi router and work-from-home desk
- [ ] Unpack books, decor and pictures last
- [ ] Sort the garage, shed and storage areas
- [ ] Anchor tall furniture and TVs to the wall if you have children — _Tip-overs of furniture and TVs are a leading cause of home injuries to young children._

## Boxes and Admin

- [ ] Flatten boxes as you empty them
- [ ] Check packing paper for small items before throwing it away
- [ ] Recycle, store or give away boxes and packing materials
- [ ] Note any damaged items and file mover's claims promptly
- [ ] Update your address with banks, employer, insurer and subscriptions
- [ ] Register with local services such as a doctor, dentist and vet
