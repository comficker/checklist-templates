# Boat Safety Checklist

A pre-departure safety routine covering life jackets, signaling devices, communication, weather and a float plan before heading out on the water.

> Free printable & editable version: [Boat Safety Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/boat-safety-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Life Jackets & Personal Safety

- [ ] Carry a properly fitting life jacket for every person aboard
- [ ] Include correctly sized life jackets for children
- [ ] Have a throwable flotation device if required
- [ ] Check life jackets for damage and working buckles
- [ ] Wear life jackets in rough water and by children

## Signaling & Distress Equipment

- [ ] Carry visual distress signals such as flares — _Check expiry dates and regional requirements_
- [ ] Have a sound-producing device like a horn or whistle
- [ ] Confirm navigation lights work for low light and night
- [ ] Carry fire extinguishers, charged and within date
- [ ] Keep a first aid kit stocked and accessible

## Communication & Navigation

- [ ] Test the VHF radio and know the emergency channel
- [ ] Carry a charged phone in a waterproof case as backup
- [ ] Have charts or a GPS chartplotter for your area
- [ ] Check the compass and confirm it reads correctly
- [ ] Confirm you know local markers and no-go zones

## Weather & Conditions

- [ ] Check the marine forecast before leaving
- [ ] Note wind, wave height, tides and visibility
- [ ] Watch for changing skies and storms while underway
- [ ] Set a turn-back time and stick to it
- [ ] Plan a route that suits the conditions and your boat

## Float Plan & Crew Briefing

- [ ] Write a float plan with route, crew and return time
- [ ] Leave the float plan with a trusted person ashore
- [ ] Brief passengers on life jacket and emergency locations
- [ ] Explain how to call for help on the radio
- [ ] Assign a second person who can operate the boat

## Boat & Fuel Readiness

- [ ] Check fuel level and carry a reserve
- [ ] Confirm the bilge pump works and bilge is clear
- [ ] Test the engine and steering at the dock
- [ ] Check the battery is charged and connections secure
- [ ] Confirm anchor, line and fenders are aboard
- [ ] Verify drain plug is installed before launching
