# ADHD Cleaning Checklist

Cleaning broken into steps short enough to start, for anyone who stalls at the size of the job rather than the work itself.

> Free printable & editable version: [ADHD Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/adhd-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Before you start (two minutes)

- [ ] Pick ONE room and close the door on the rest
- [ ] Set a timer for 15 minutes — _The timer is permission to stop, not a target to beat. Stopping when it goes off is the point._
- [ ] Put on music or a podcast you already like
- [ ] Carry in one bin bag, one laundry basket, one box for things that live elsewhere
- [ ] Fill a glass of water and bring it with you
- [ ] Ask someone to sit in the room or stay on a video call — _Working alongside another person helps many people start. It does not matter what they are doing._

## Round one: rubbish only

- [ ] Walk the room and put only rubbish in the bag
- [ ] Do not sort, do not decide, do not keep anything you are unsure about
- [ ] Take the bag out when it is full or when you have gone round once

## Round two: dishes and cups

- [ ] Collect every cup, plate and bottle onto one tray
- [ ] Carry the tray to the kitchen
- [ ] Put them in the dishwasher or leave them in the sink in water — _Washing up is a separate job. Moving them out of the room is the win here._

## Round three: laundry

- [ ] Put every piece of dirty clothing in the basket
- [ ] Put clean clothes in one pile on the bed, unfolded
- [ ] Start a wash if the machine is empty

## Round four: things that live elsewhere

- [ ] Put everything that belongs in another room into the box
- [ ] Do not leave the room to deliver them yet — _Leaving mid-task is where the session usually ends._
- [ ] Deliver the box at the very end, in one trip

## Round five: one surface

- [ ] Pick the surface you see first when you walk in
- [ ] Clear it completely
- [ ] Wipe it
- [ ] Put back only what belongs there — _One clear surface changes how the whole room reads, which is why it beats cleaning three things halfway._

## If it is too much today

- [ ] Do the five-minute version: rubbish and dishes only
- [ ] Clear one square metre of floor and stop
- [ ] Make the bed and count that as done
- [ ] Set the timer for five minutes instead of fifteen
- [ ] Leave the list where you can see it and come back tomorrow — _A half-done room is not a failed attempt. It is a room with less in it than yesterday._

## Making it easier next time

- [ ] Keep a bin in every room
- [ ] Put a laundry basket where clothes actually land
- [ ] Keep cleaning supplies in the room that needs them, not in one cupboard
- [ ] Use open baskets rather than lidded boxes — _A lid is an extra step, and extra steps are where things get put down instead of away._
- [ ] Pick one daily reset: clear the kitchen counter, or the floor, or the bed
- [ ] Attach the reset to something you already do, like after dinner
