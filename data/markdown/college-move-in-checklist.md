# College Move-In Checklist

A step-by-step checklist for a smooth college move-in day.

> Free printable & editable version: [College Move-In Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/college-move-in-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Before move-in day

- [ ] Confirm your assigned move-in date and time window.
- [ ] Review parking, traffic and unloading instructions. — _Many schools enforce strict time slots — plan to arrive on time._
- [ ] Map your route and a backup in case of closures.
- [ ] Coordinate large or shared items with your roommate.
- [ ] Label every box with your name and room number.
- [ ] Charge your phone and any devices the night before.

## Pack the vehicle

- [ ] Load heavy and bulky items into the vehicle first.
- [ ] Keep your first-night box easy to reach.
- [ ] Bring a hand truck or dolly if you have one.
- [ ] Pack a small toolkit for assembling furniture.
- [ ] Bring water, snacks and comfortable shoes.
- [ ] Keep documents and keys in a bag with you.

## Arrival and check-in

- [ ] Arrive within your assigned move-in window.
- [ ] Check in and pick up your room key or access card.
- [ ] Collect any move-in packet or paperwork.
- [ ] Note where to park and the unloading time limit.
- [ ] Locate elevators, stairs and the nearest entrance.
- [ ] Find out where move-in carts are available.

## Unload and carry up

- [ ] Unload the vehicle quickly to free the spot.
- [ ] Move heavy items first while energy is high.
- [ ] Stage boxes in the room before unpacking.
- [ ] Keep walkways and doorways clear for others.
- [ ] Have a helper guard items during trips.
- [ ] Break down empty boxes and recycle them.

## Set up the room

- [ ] Make the bed first so you can rest later.
- [ ] Set up your desk and charge your devices.
- [ ] Unpack toiletries and your first-night essentials.
- [ ] Arrange storage and put clothes away.
- [ ] Test electronics, outlets and the internet.
- [ ] Add a few personal touches to feel at home.

## Paperwork and wrap-up

- [ ] Complete the room condition or inventory form. — _Document existing damage to protect your deposit._
- [ ] Photograph any damage you find on arrival.
- [ ] Save housing, RA and maintenance contacts.
- [ ] Confirm your meal plan and ID card work.
- [ ] Note quiet hours, laundry and trash rules.
- [ ] Say goodbye to family and attend any welcome events.
