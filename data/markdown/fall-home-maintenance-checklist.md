# Fall Home Maintenance Checklist

A fall home maintenance checklist of autumn tasks that prepare your home for winter.

> Free printable & editable version: [Fall Home Maintenance Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/fall-home-maintenance-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Heating & HVAC

- [ ] Schedule a professional furnace or heat-pump tune-up
- [ ] Replace the furnace filter and stock spares for the season — _Check 1-inch filters monthly through heating season_
- [ ] Test the heating system on a cool day before you need it
- [ ] Bleed radiators or check boiler pressure on hydronic systems
- [ ] Reverse ceiling fans to clockwise to circulate warm air down
- [ ] Confirm the thermostat works and replace its batteries

## Gutters, Roof & Exterior

- [ ] Clean gutters and downspouts after most leaves have fallen
- [ ] Inspect the roof from the ground for loose or missing shingles
- [ ] Check flashing and seals around chimneys, vents and skylights
- [ ] Confirm downspouts direct water at least three feet from the foundation
- [ ] Rake leaves away from the foundation and clear window wells
- [ ] Trim dead branches that could fall under snow or ice load

## Plumbing & Water

- [ ] Drain and shut off outdoor faucets and sprinkler systems
- [ ] Disconnect, drain and store garden hoses
- [ ] Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas like the garage and crawl space
- [ ] Test the sump pump before winter storms and snowmelt
- [ ] Locate the main water shut-off in case a pipe bursts

## Seal & Weatherproof

- [ ] Apply weatherstripping to doors and seal drafty windows
- [ ] Caulk gaps and cracks where pipes and wires enter the house
- [ ] Check attic insulation depth and add more if it's thin
- [ ] Install draft stoppers under exterior doors
- [ ] Inspect and reverse or remove window AC units

## Fireplace & Safety

- [ ] Hire a certified chimney sweep before lighting the first fire
- [ ] Test smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms and replace old batteries — _CO risk rises in fall and winter with fuel-burning heat_
- [ ] Check the fireplace damper opens and closes fully
- [ ] Inspect and recharge the kitchen fire extinguisher
- [ ] Stock firewood in a dry, covered spot away from the house

## Yard & Outdoor Prep

- [ ] Aerate, fertilize and overseed the lawn before frost
- [ ] Clean and store outdoor furniture, grills and tools
- [ ] Service and store the lawn mower; ready the snow blower
- [ ] Stock up on ice melt and check that snow shovels are usable
- [ ] Empty and store pots; protect tender plants from frost
