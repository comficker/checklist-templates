# Move Out Cleaning Checklist

An end-of-tenancy move out cleaning checklist to pass inspection and secure your deposit.

> Free printable & editable version: [Move Out Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/move-out-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Whole-Home Prep

- [ ] Remove all furniture, belongings, and trash from every room
- [ ] Patch small nail holes and touch up scuffed paint if your lease requires it
- [ ] Dust ceiling corners, fans, and light fixtures throughout
- [ ] Wipe down all walls, doors, frames, and light switches
- [ ] Clean air vents and replace HVAC filters
- [ ] Take dated photos once each room is cleaned — _Photos protect you if there is any dispute over the deposit._

## Kitchen

- [ ] Clean the oven interior, racks, broiler, and door glass
- [ ] Pull out the stove and fridge to clean behind and underneath
- [ ] Empty, defrost, and wipe the refrigerator and freezer
- [ ] Degrease the range hood and replace or wash the filter
- [ ] Empty and wipe inside all cabinets and drawers
- [ ] Scrub the sink, descale the faucet, and clean the disposal
- [ ] Wipe countertops, the backsplash, and cabinet fronts

## Bathrooms

- [ ] Scrub and disinfect the toilet, including the base and behind it
- [ ] Deep clean the shower, tub, and remove all soap scum
- [ ] Scrub grout lines and treat any mildew
- [ ] Descale the showerhead, faucets, and fixtures
- [ ] Clean the mirror, vanity, and inside the cabinets
- [ ] Clear and disinfect all drains
- [ ] Clean the exhaust fan cover and mop the floor

## Bedrooms and Living Areas

- [ ] Wipe down baseboards, trim, and door frames
- [ ] Clean inside and outside of all closets and shelving
- [ ] Dust and wipe window sills, ledges, and built-ins
- [ ] Remove any wall hooks, mounts, and adhesive residue
- [ ] Spot-clean walls and remove scuff marks
- [ ] Vacuum or steam-clean carpets to lift stains

## Windows, Doors and Fixtures

- [ ] Wash interior windows and wipe them streak-free
- [ ] Vacuum and wipe window tracks and sills
- [ ] Dust blinds and launder or wipe curtains if they stay
- [ ] Clean light fixtures and replace any burnt-out bulbs
- [ ] Wipe down all doors, handles, and frames
- [ ] Clean mirrors and any glass surfaces

## Floors and Final Walkthrough

- [ ] Sweep and vacuum every room, including closets and corners
- [ ] Mop all hard floors and spot-treat stains
- [ ] Steam-clean or shampoo carpets if required by the lease
- [ ] Clean and disinfect any outdoor patio, balcony, or entry
- [ ] Remove all trash and leave bins clean
- [ ] Do a final inspection room by room against your lease checklist
