# Monthly Cleaning Checklist

A monthly cleaning checklist of deeper tasks that go beyond the weekly routine.

> Free printable & editable version: [Monthly Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/monthly-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Dust and Detail

- [ ] Wipe down baseboards and trim throughout the home
- [ ] Dust ceiling fan blades and light fixtures
- [ ] Vacuum and wipe air vents and return grilles
- [ ] Dust door frames, picture frames, and the tops of cabinets
- [ ] Remove cobwebs from ceiling corners and high spots
- [ ] Wipe down light switch plates and outlet covers

## Kitchen Appliances

- [ ] Clean the inside of the microwave and remove the turntable to wash it
- [ ] Wipe down the fridge interior and discard expired food
- [ ] Run a cleaning cycle on the dishwasher and clear the filter — _A cup of vinegar on the top rack freshens a dishwasher between deep cleans._
- [ ] Descale the kettle and coffee maker
- [ ] Degrease the range hood and wipe the filter
- [ ] Wipe down cabinet fronts and the backsplash

## Bathrooms

- [ ] Wash the shower curtain or wipe down the glass door tracks
- [ ] Scrub grout lines and treat any early mildew spots
- [ ] Descale the showerhead and faucets
- [ ] Clean the exhaust fan cover and clear dust from the vent
- [ ] Disinfect drains in the sink, tub, and shower
- [ ] Wipe down cabinet interiors and reorganize toiletries

## Bedrooms and Soft Furnishings

- [ ] Wash mattress protectors and pillow protectors
- [ ] Vacuum the mattress and rotate or flip it
- [ ] Launder throw blankets, cushion covers, and decorative pillows
- [ ] Vacuum under the bed and behind the headboard
- [ ] Wipe down closet shelves and reorganize drawers

## Living Areas and Furniture

- [ ] Move sofas and chairs to vacuum underneath
- [ ] Vacuum upholstery, including under and between cushions
- [ ] Dust and polish wood furniture, including legs
- [ ] Wipe down electronics, remotes, and cable boxes
- [ ] Spot-clean stains on rugs and upholstery
- [ ] Clean window sills and wipe down blinds

## Whole-Home and Utility

- [ ] Replace or wash HVAC filters
- [ ] Wipe down the washer and dryer and clean the lint trap thoroughly
- [ ] Vacuum and wipe baseboards in hallways and stairs
- [ ] Clean and disinfect trash and recycling bins
- [ ] Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors and change batteries if needed
- [ ] Vacuum or wash entry mats and doormats
