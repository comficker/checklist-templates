# Hotel Night Audit Checklist

A shift checklist for the hotel night auditor, from the evening handover to the system date roll and the morning report.

> Free printable & editable version: [Hotel Night Audit Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/night-audit-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Start of Shift

- [ ] Get a handover from the evening shift on open issues and VIPs
- [ ] Count the cash drawer with the outgoing clerk and both sign the count — _Never accept a drawer you have not counted yourself._
- [ ] Review today's arrivals still due and any late check-in notes
- [ ] Read the log book for guest requests, complaints and wake-up calls

## Check Accounts

- [ ] Post any outlet charges not yet on guest folios, such as restaurant or bar
- [ ] Check rates on in-house folios match the reservation
- [ ] Check that discounts, packages and comp rooms are approved and coded right
- [ ] Review the high balance report and flag guests over their authorization
- [ ] Check that departures due today are checked out or extended

## Room Status

- [ ] Compare the PMS room status with the housekeeping report
- [ ] Investigate rooms the PMS shows vacant but housekeeping found occupied — _A room that is occupied but shown vacant in the PMS can be a guest staying without paying, or a check-in the desk missed. Check it before you post room charges._
- [ ] Investigate rooms shown occupied but found vacant (possible skips)
- [ ] Correct room status in the PMS and note each change in the log

## No-Shows and Reservations

- [ ] Run the list of arrivals that did not check in
- [ ] Charge guaranteed no-shows per the hotel's no-show policy — _Check for a cancellation number or a message from the guest before you charge._
- [ ] Cancel non-guaranteed reservations that did not arrive
- [ ] Check tomorrow's arrivals for guarantees, deposits and special requests

## Cash and Card Balancing

- [ ] Balance the cash drawer against the PMS cashier report
- [ ] Match card payments in the PMS to the card terminal totals
- [ ] Settle the credit card batch and keep the settlement report — _If the PMS and the terminal do not match, find the difference before you settle. Common causes are a duplicate swipe or a payment posted to the wrong room._
- [ ] Prepare the deposit and drop it in the safe per hotel policy

## Post Charges and Close the Day

- [ ] Post room and tax charges to all in-house folios
- [ ] Review the posting report for missing or doubled charges
- [ ] Print or save the backup reports before the roll — _Keep the in-house guest list, room status, arrivals and guest ledger on paper in case the PMS goes down._
- [ ] Run the end-of-day roll to change the system date — _Make sure no other user is posting at the desk while the roll runs._

## Reports and Morning Handover

- [ ] Run the daily revenue report and check room, tax and other revenue
- [ ] Record occupancy, ADR and RevPAR for the day
- [ ] Run the guest ledger and city ledger balance reports
- [ ] Send or file the audit pack for the general manager and accounting
- [ ] Count the drawer with the morning clerk and both sign the count
- [ ] Brief the morning shift on issues, high balances and early departures
