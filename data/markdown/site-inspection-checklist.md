# Site Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting a work or construction site.

> Free printable & editable version: [Site Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/site-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Site access and signage

- [ ] Confirm site perimeter fencing is intact and secure with no gaps.
- [ ] Check that gates and entry points are controlled and lockable.
- [ ] Verify warning, hazard and mandatory PPE signs are posted and legible.
- [ ] Confirm emergency contact and site rules are displayed at entrances.
- [ ] Check pedestrian and vehicle routes are separated and clearly marked.
- [ ] Verify visitor and contractor sign-in procedures are in place.

## Safety and PPE

- [ ] Confirm all workers are wearing required hard hats, hi-vis and footwear.
- [ ] Check that task-specific PPE is available and in good condition.
- [ ] Verify first aid kits are stocked and a trained first aider is on site.
- [ ] Confirm a current site-specific safety plan is available and followed.
- [ ] Check that permits to work are posted for hot work, confined space or excavation.
- [ ] Verify recent safety briefings or toolbox talks have been recorded.

## Hazard control

- [ ] Inspect open edges, holes and excavations for guarding or covers. — _Fall protection is required where workers can fall to a lower level per OSHA thresholds._
- [ ] Check that electrical cords, panels and GFCI protection are sound and dry.
- [ ] Verify flammable and hazardous materials are stored and labeled correctly.
- [ ] Confirm overhead loads and lifting zones are barricaded off.
- [ ] Check ladders and scaffolds are tagged, stable and used correctly.
- [ ] Identify and control any slip, trip or fall hazards on walkways.

## Housekeeping and storage

- [ ] Confirm walkways, stairs and exits are clear of debris and materials.
- [ ] Check that waste is segregated and bins are not overflowing.
- [ ] Verify materials are stacked safely and not blocking access.
- [ ] Confirm tools and equipment are stored when not in use.
- [ ] Check spill kits are available and any spills are cleaned up.
- [ ] Verify standing water and mud are managed to prevent slips.

## Equipment and plant

- [ ] Confirm plant and machinery have current inspection tags.
- [ ] Check guarding is fitted on moving parts and not bypassed.
- [ ] Verify operators are trained and authorized for the equipment they use.
- [ ] Inspect lifting gear and slings for damage and current certification.
- [ ] Confirm fuel and gas cylinders are secured and stored upright.
- [ ] Check fire extinguishers are present, charged and accessible.

## Emergency and progress

- [ ] Confirm escape routes and muster points are clear and signposted.
- [ ] Check emergency lighting and alarms function where installed.
- [ ] Verify access for emergency vehicles is maintained.
- [ ] Review whether work is progressing safely to plan and schedule.
- [ ] Record any defects with a responsible person and due date.
- [ ] Sign, date and file the completed inspection record.
