# Lockout/Tagout Checklist

A LOTO hazardous-energy control check covering shutdown, isolation, locking and verification.

> Free printable & editable version: [Lockout/Tagout Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/lockout-tagout-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Preparation and Identification

- [ ] Confirm the equipment has a documented energy control procedure.
- [ ] Identify all energy sources: electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal and chemical.
- [ ] Locate every energy-isolating device for the equipment. — _Stored energy such as springs and capacitors must also be identified._
- [ ] Verify the authorized employee is trained for this equipment.
- [ ] Notify all affected employees before shutting the equipment down.

## Shutdown and Isolation

- [ ] Shut down the equipment using its normal stopping procedure.
- [ ] Operate each energy-isolating device to isolate the equipment.
- [ ] Confirm valves, breakers and disconnects are in the safe position.
- [ ] Block or restrain any parts that could move under stored energy. — _Do not rely on control switches alone to isolate energy._
- [ ] Isolate connected lines carrying gas, steam or fluids.

## Locking and Tagging

- [ ] Apply a personal lock to each energy-isolating device.
- [ ] Attach a tag identifying the worker, date and reason for lockout.
- [ ] Confirm each authorized worker applies their own lock for group work.
- [ ] Use a lockout hasp or group lockbox when multiple locks are needed. — _Each worker must control their own lock and key._
- [ ] Verify locks and tags are durable, standardized and clearly marked.

## Release Stored Energy and Verify

- [ ] Release or restrain residual and stored energy safely.
- [ ] Bleed pressure, discharge capacitors and dissipate thermal energy.
- [ ] Attempt to start the equipment with controls to confirm isolation.
- [ ] Return controls to off after the test. — _Verify zero energy with the appropriate instrument before contact._
- [ ] Confirm a zero-energy state before any work begins.

## Servicing and Restoration

- [ ] Perform the maintenance with locks and tags in place.
- [ ] Confirm the work area is clear of tools and personnel before restart.
- [ ] Verify guards and safety devices are reinstalled.
- [ ] Remove each lock only by the worker who applied it.
- [ ] Notify affected employees before re-energizing the equipment.
- [ ] Re-energize and test the equipment for safe operation.

## Program and Records

- [ ] Confirm machine-specific procedures are written and accessible.
- [ ] Verify authorized and affected employees are trained and retrained as needed.
- [ ] Check that periodic inspections of the LOTO program are documented.
- [ ] Confirm contractors follow a coordinated energy control procedure.
- [ ] Important: this is general guidance only and does not replace OSHA regulations, formal training or your employer's energy control procedures; confirm requirements with a qualified person.
