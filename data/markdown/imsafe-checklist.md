# IMSAFE Checklist

A pre-flight self-assessment for pilots that walks through each IMSAFE letter, followed by a short PAVE risk check.

> Free printable & editable version: [IMSAFE Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/imsafe-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## I – Illness

- [ ] Ask: Do I have any symptoms, even a mild cold?
- [ ] Check for a blocked nose or ears that could hurt during descent — _Congestion can stop your ears and sinuses from clearing as pressure changes. This can cause severe pain or a burst eardrum._
- [ ] Ask: Is there any condition that would make me fail my medical?
- [ ] Ask: Have I been scuba diving in the last 24 hours? — _The AIM advises waiting at least 12 hours after diving before flying up to 8,000 ft, and 24 hours after any dive that needed a controlled ascent or before flying higher._

## M – Medication

- [ ] List every prescription and over-the-counter drug taken recently
- [ ] Do not fly on the first doses of a new drug until you know how it affects you
- [ ] Check labels for warnings about drowsiness or operating machinery
- [ ] Wait five dosing intervals after a sedating drug before flying — _Diphenhydramine, found in many sleep aids and allergy pills, is a common example._
- [ ] Ask: Is the condition I am treating itself a reason not to fly?

## S – Stress

- [ ] Ask: Am I under pressure from work, money, health or family?
- [ ] Ask: Is anything on my mind that will pull my attention from flying?
- [ ] Ask: Have I had a big life change lately, such as a move, new job or loss?
- [ ] Ask: Am I rushing to meet a deadline for this flight?

## A – Alcohol

- [ ] Confirm at least 8 hours since your last drink (14 CFR 91.17) — _This is the legal minimum. Many pilots use a personal limit of 12 to 24 hours._
- [ ] Confirm your blood alcohol is below 0.04 percent
- [ ] Ask: Do I have any hangover symptoms such as headache or nausea?
- [ ] Ask: Am I still under the influence of anything I took last night?

## F – Fatigue

- [ ] Ask: Did I get a full night of good sleep?
- [ ] Ask: How long have I been awake, and how long will I be by landing?
- [ ] Ask: Will this flight run into my normal sleep time?
- [ ] Ask: Am I yawning, losing focus or rereading things?

## E – Emotion (and Eating)

- [ ] Ask: Am I upset, angry or grieving after a recent event?
- [ ] Ask: Am I feeling overconfident or that I have something to prove?
- [ ] Ask: Have I eaten a proper meal in the last few hours?
- [ ] Bring water and a snack for flights over an hour — _Low blood sugar and mild dehydration both slow thinking and can feel like fatigue._

## PAVE Risk Check

- [ ] Pilot: Am I current, proficient and IMSAFE for this flight?
- [ ] Aircraft: Is it airworthy, fueled, within weight and balance, and equipped for this flight?
- [ ] enVironment: Are weather, terrain, airports and daylight within my personal minimums?
- [ ] External pressures: Am I willing to delay or cancel if things change? — _Have a backup plan, such as a hotel, rental car or later flight, so get-there-itis does not decide for you._
