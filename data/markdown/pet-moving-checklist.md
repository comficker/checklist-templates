# Pet Moving Checklist

An owner-focused checklist for moving house with a pet, covering records, ID, travel and settling in.

> Free printable & editable version: [Pet Moving Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/pet-moving-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Vet & Health Records

- [ ] Request copies of your pet's full medical records
- [ ] Confirm vaccinations are current
- [ ] Ask your vet about travel, sedation or motion sickness — _Never give human medication without veterinary advice_
- [ ] Refill any ongoing medications before the move
- [ ] Research a new vet near your future home

## Identification Updates

- [ ] Update your microchip registration with the new address and phone
- [ ] Replace ID tags with current contact details
- [ ] Update any pet license or registration with local authorities
- [ ] Take a recent photo in case your pet goes missing during the move

## Travel Preparation

- [ ] Acclimate your pet to the carrier or crate in advance
- [ ] Pack a travel kit with food, water, bowls and waste bags
- [ ] Plan rest, water and bathroom stops for long drives
- [ ] Confirm airline or transport rules if flying or shipping the pet
- [ ] Never leave a pet alone in a parked vehicle

## Moving Day Safety

- [ ] Confine your pet to a closed, quiet room or arrange boarding
- [ ] Place a clear sign on the door so movers keep it shut
- [ ] Keep collar and ID tags on the pet at all times
- [ ] Pack a familiar blanket, bed or toy to bring on the journey
- [ ] Feed lightly before travel to reduce nausea

## Settling Into the New Home

- [ ] Set up one secured, quiet room with familiar items first
- [ ] Pet-proof the home and check for escape routes and hazards
- [ ] Introduce the rest of the home gradually
- [ ] Keep feeding, walk and sleep routines consistent
- [ ] Update walking routes and locate the nearest emergency vet
