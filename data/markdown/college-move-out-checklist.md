# College Move-Out Checklist

An end-of-year checklist for cleaning, packing and moving out of a dorm.

> Free printable & editable version: [College Move-Out Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/college-move-out-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Plan ahead

- [ ] Confirm the exact move-out deadline and check-out hours. — _Late departures often trigger daily fees — confirm the cutoff early._
- [ ] Review the check-out and cleaning requirements.
- [ ] Reserve a vehicle, storage or shipping if needed.
- [ ] Coordinate timing and tasks with your roommate.
- [ ] Gather boxes, tape, bags and labels in advance.
- [ ] Schedule any travel home around the deadline.

## Sort belongings

- [ ] Separate items into keep, store, donate and trash.
- [ ] Decide what goes home versus into summer storage.
- [ ] Sell or give away things you no longer need.
- [ ] Set aside donations for a campus or local drop-off.
- [ ] Empty the fridge and toss expired food.
- [ ] Return any borrowed or rented items.

## Pack and label

- [ ] Start with off-season and rarely used items.
- [ ] Pack heavy items in small, sturdy boxes.
- [ ] Wrap fragile items and electronics carefully.
- [ ] Keep an essentials bag for your final days.
- [ ] Label each box with contents and destination.
- [ ] Keep important documents and valuables with you.

## Clean the room

- [ ] Remove all trash, tape and wall hooks.
- [ ] Dust surfaces and wipe down furniture.
- [ ] Clean the desk, shelves, drawers and closet.
- [ ] Sweep, vacuum and mop the floor.
- [ ] Clean any sink, microwave or fridge in the room.
- [ ] Leave the room as close to move-in condition as possible.

## Ship or store

- [ ] Move stored items to a storage unit or service.
- [ ] Ship boxes home if driving isn't practical.
- [ ] Confirm pickup or drop-off times for any service.
- [ ] Insure or photograph valuable shipped items.
- [ ] Keep tracking numbers and receipts.
- [ ] Load your vehicle the night before if possible.

## Final check-out

- [ ] Do a final walk-through for forgotten items.
- [ ] Photograph the empty, cleaned room.
- [ ] Complete the official check-out inspection.
- [ ] Return your room key or access card on time. — _Lost keys often mean a re-keying charge against your deposit._
- [ ] Update your mailing address with the school.
- [ ] Confirm your deposit return process and timeline.
