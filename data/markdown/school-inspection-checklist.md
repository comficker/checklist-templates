# School Inspection Checklist

A facility inspection checklist covering safety, maintenance, compliance and grounds.

> Free printable & editable version: [School Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/school-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Safety systems

- [ ] Test fire alarms and smoke detectors.
- [ ] Inspect extinguishers for charge, tags and access.
- [ ] Check sprinkler and suppression systems.
- [ ] Confirm emergency lighting and exit signs work.
- [ ] Verify exits and fire doors open and latch. — _Confirm no exits are blocked or chained._
- [ ] Check first-aid kits and AED units are stocked.

## Building maintenance

- [ ] Inspect the roof, gutters and ceilings for leaks.
- [ ] Check HVAC operation and replace filters.
- [ ] Test plumbing for leaks and water pressure.
- [ ] Inspect electrical panels, outlets and wiring.
- [ ] Check doors, windows and locks for function.
- [ ] Examine floors, stairs and railings for damage.

## Compliance and documentation

- [ ] Confirm permits and certificates are current.
- [ ] Review inspection records and prior findings.
- [ ] Check accessibility features meet requirements.
- [ ] Verify safety data sheets are accessible.
- [ ] Confirm occupancy limits and signage are posted.
- [ ] Log all findings with date and assigned owner.

## Sanitation

- [ ] Inspect restrooms for cleanliness and supplies.
- [ ] Check kitchen and cafeteria food-safety conditions.
- [ ] Confirm trash and waste are managed properly.
- [ ] Look for signs of pests or infestation.
- [ ] Verify cleaning chemicals are stored safely.
- [ ] Check ventilation and air quality in key areas.

## Grounds and exterior

- [ ] Inspect playground equipment for damage and wear.
- [ ] Check walkways and parking for trip hazards.
- [ ] Confirm exterior lighting works after dark.
- [ ] Examine fencing, gates and perimeter security.
- [ ] Clear drainage and check for standing water.
- [ ] Verify signage and markings are visible.
