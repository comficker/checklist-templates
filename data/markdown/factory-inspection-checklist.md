# Factory Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting a factory or production plant.

> Free printable & editable version: [Factory Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/factory-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Machinery and guarding

- [ ] Confirm guards are fitted on moving parts and not bypassed. — _Machine guarding is required by OSHA; never operate with a defeated interlock._
- [ ] Check emergency stops are accessible and function on test.
- [ ] Verify lockout/tagout procedures and devices are available.
- [ ] Inspect machines for leaks, unusual noise or vibration.
- [ ] Confirm machines have current maintenance and inspection records.
- [ ] Check operators are trained and authorized for each machine.

## Workplace safety

- [ ] Confirm required PPE is worn and available at each station.
- [ ] Check noise, dust and fume controls are working.
- [ ] Verify electrical panels are labeled, closed and accessible.
- [ ] Confirm chemicals are labeled, stored and have safety data sheets.
- [ ] Check first aid, eyewash and emergency showers are stocked.
- [ ] Verify fire extinguishers are charged, tagged and accessible.

## Quality control

- [ ] Confirm work instructions and specifications are available.
- [ ] Check measuring and test equipment is calibrated and in date.
- [ ] Verify in-process inspection and sampling are being performed.
- [ ] Confirm nonconforming product is segregated and labeled.
- [ ] Check materials are within shelf life and stored correctly.
- [ ] Verify batch and traceability records are complete.

## Housekeeping

- [ ] Confirm aisles and walkways are clear and marked.
- [ ] Check floors for spills, debris and trip hazards.
- [ ] Verify waste and scrap are removed and segregated.
- [ ] Confirm tools and materials are stored when not in use.
- [ ] Check lighting is adequate across the production area.
- [ ] Verify drains and ventilation are clear and functioning.

## Equipment and utilities

- [ ] Confirm compressed air systems and lines are leak-free and secured.
- [ ] Check pressure vessels and boilers show current inspection.
- [ ] Verify conveyors have guarding, stops and clear access.
- [ ] Inspect lifting equipment and slings for damage and certification.
- [ ] Confirm forklifts and trucks have daily pre-use checks.
- [ ] Check backup power and critical utilities operate on test.

## Compliance and records

- [ ] Confirm permits, licenses and certificates are current.
- [ ] Check environmental controls and waste handling are compliant.
- [ ] Verify training and competency records are up to date.
- [ ] Review previous findings for completed corrective actions.
- [ ] Record each defect with location, severity and photo.
- [ ] Assign owners and due dates, then sign, date and file.
