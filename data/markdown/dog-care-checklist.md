# Dog Care Checklist

A dog care checklist organizing daily, weekly, and monthly routines that keep a dog healthy and happy.

> Free printable & editable version: [Dog Care Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/dog-care-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Daily care

- [ ] Serve measured meals at consistent times and refresh water.
- [ ] Provide daily exercise and a walk suited to your dog's energy.
- [ ] Take regular potty breaks throughout the day.
- [ ] Spend time on play, training, and bonding.
- [ ] Pick up waste and keep eating and resting areas clean.
- [ ] Check mood, appetite, and behavior for anything unusual.

## Weekly care

- [ ] Brush your dog's coat based on its type and shedding.
- [ ] Wash bedding and clean food and water bowls thoroughly.
- [ ] Check ears, eyes, and paws for dirt or irritation.
- [ ] Practice training cues to keep skills sharp.
- [ ] Inspect collar, harness, and ID tag for wear.
- [ ] Plan longer outings or enrichment for the week.

## Monthly care

- [ ] Give parasite prevention for fleas, ticks, and worms on schedule. — _Follow your veterinarian's recommended products and timing._
- [ ] Weigh your dog and note any changes.
- [ ] Trim nails if they have grown too long.
- [ ] Review and refill any medications or supplements.
- [ ] Restock food, treats, and supplies before they run out.
- [ ] Confirm ID and microchip details are current.

## Feeding and nutrition

- [ ] Feed a quality, age-appropriate dog food in measured portions.
- [ ] Provide clean, fresh water at all times.
- [ ] Limit treats and avoid foods toxic to dogs.
- [ ] Transition any food change gradually over several days.
- [ ] Adjust portions for activity, age, and weight.
- [ ] Note loss of appetite or stomach upset for your vet.

## Grooming and hygiene

- [ ] Brush regularly to reduce mats and shedding.
- [ ] Bathe as needed with a dog-safe shampoo.
- [ ] Clean ears and check for odor or buildup.
- [ ] Brush teeth or use vet-recommended dental care.
- [ ] Keep nails trimmed to a comfortable length.
- [ ] Check skin and coat for fleas, ticks, and lumps.

## Health and wellness

- [ ] Keep up with routine wellness exams and vaccinations.
- [ ] Watch energy, appetite, weight, and bathroom habits.
- [ ] Give prescribed medications exactly as directed.
- [ ] Provide mental enrichment and avoid boredom.
- [ ] Keep emergency vet contacts handy.
- [ ] Important: This is general information, not veterinary advice. Always consult a veterinarian about your dog's health, diet, and medications.
