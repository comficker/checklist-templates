# Car Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting your own car's condition and safety.

> Free printable & editable version: [Car Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/car-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Exterior and body

- [ ] Walk around the car checking for dents, rust, cracks or fresh damage.
- [ ] Confirm the license plates are present, secure and readable.
- [ ] Check that doors, hood and trunk open, close and latch securely.
- [ ] Inspect the windshield and windows for chips or cracks in the line of sight.
- [ ] Check that mirrors are intact, secure and adjust properly.
- [ ] Look under the car for fresh fluid leaks or hanging components.

## Lights and signals

- [ ] Test both low and high beam headlights for output and aim.
- [ ] Check brake lights and the center high-mount stop lamp. — _Use a wall reflection or a helper to confirm brake lights at the rear._
- [ ] Verify front and rear turn signals and hazard lights flash correctly.
- [ ] Test reverse lights and the rear license plate light.
- [ ] Check parking, fog and daytime running lights where fitted.
- [ ] Confirm no warning lights stay lit on the dashboard after start-up.

## Tires and wheels

- [ ] Check tire pressure on all tires against the door-jamb sticker.
- [ ] Measure tread depth and confirm it is above the legal minimum.
- [ ] Look for uneven wear, cuts, bulges or embedded objects.
- [ ] Confirm all lug nuts are present and wheel covers are secure.
- [ ] Inspect the spare tire's pressure and condition.
- [ ] Verify the TPMS light is off or address the warning.

## Brakes and steering

- [ ] Test the brake pedal for firm, consistent feel without sinking.
- [ ] Listen for grinding or squealing when braking. — _Grinding usually means worn pads; have the brakes inspected immediately._
- [ ] Confirm the parking brake holds the car on an incline.
- [ ] Check the steering for excessive play, pulling or vibration.
- [ ] Verify the ABS and brake warning lights are off.
- [ ] Listen for clunks over bumps that suggest worn suspension.

## Fluids and under the hood

- [ ] Check engine oil level and condition on the dipstick.
- [ ] Verify coolant level in the reservoir when the engine is cold.
- [ ] Check brake fluid and power steering fluid levels.
- [ ] Top up windshield washer fluid.
- [ ] Inspect belts and hoses for cracks, fraying or leaks.
- [ ] Check the battery terminals are clean, tight and corrosion-free.

## Interior and safety equipment

- [ ] Test the horn for a clear, loud sound.
- [ ] Check that wipers clear the glass and washers spray properly.
- [ ] Confirm all seatbelts latch, retract and are undamaged.
- [ ] Verify the defroster, heater and ventilation work.
- [ ] Check that warning, oil and fuel gauges read correctly.
- [ ] Confirm a warning triangle, first-aid kit and required items are aboard.
