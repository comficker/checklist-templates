# First Aid Kit Checklist

A checklist of supplies to stock in a home and car first aid kit, organized by category.

> Free printable & editable version: [First Aid Kit Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/first-aid-kit-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Wound Care and Bandages

- [ ] Adhesive bandages in assorted sizes
- [ ] Sterile gauze pads and rolls
- [ ] Medical tape
- [ ] Antiseptic wipes or solution
- [ ] Antibiotic or wound-care ointment
- [ ] Elastic wrap for sprains

## Tools and Protection

- [ ] Disposable gloves
- [ ] Scissors and tweezers
- [ ] A digital thermometer
- [ ] Safety pins
- [ ] An instant cold pack
- [ ] A small flashlight with spare batteries

## Medicines and Basics

- [ ] General pain and fever relief
- [ ] Allergy relief
- [ ] Antacids or stomach-settling items
- [ ] Burn gel or dressings
- [ ] Hand sanitizer — _Keep all medicines in date and suitable for your household_

## Car Kit Extras

- [ ] A foil emergency blanket
- [ ] Bottled water and a snack
- [ ] A reflective vest or warning triangle
- [ ] Work gloves
- [ ] A phone charger or power bank

## Information and Maintenance

- [ ] A first aid instruction card or booklet
- [ ] A list of emergency contacts
- [ ] Notes on any family allergies or conditions
- [ ] Check expiry dates and replace expired items
- [ ] Restock anything used and review twice a year — _For emergencies, call your local emergency number such as 911_

## Important

- [ ] This checklist is general information only and is not medical advice; a first aid kit does not replace professional care, so for serious injuries or emergencies call your local emergency number and follow guidance from trained responders — _Consider a recognized first aid training course_
