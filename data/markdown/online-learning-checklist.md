# Online Learning Checklist

An online learning checklist that helps students succeed in online and virtual classes.

> Free printable & editable version: [Online Learning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/online-learning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Set up your tech

- [ ] Confirm your computer and internet connection are reliable.
- [ ] Test your webcam, microphone and speakers.
- [ ] Install the required learning platform and apps.
- [ ] Bookmark the course site, syllabus and login pages.
- [ ] Have a backup plan if your connection drops. — _Know a nearby Wi-Fi spot or keep mobile data ready._

## Organize your schedule

- [ ] Add all live sessions and class times to your calendar.
- [ ] Note when recorded lectures and materials are released.
- [ ] Block regular study time for each course.
- [ ] Set reminders for assignments and exams.
- [ ] Plan around any time-zone differences.

## Create a focus space

- [ ] Choose a quiet, dedicated spot for studying.
- [ ] Keep your materials and charger within reach.
- [ ] Silence phone notifications during class and study.
- [ ] Close unrelated tabs and apps while you work.
- [ ] Use focused work blocks with short breaks.

## Participate actively

- [ ] Join live sessions on time and stay engaged.
- [ ] Take notes just as you would in person.
- [ ] Ask questions in chat or by unmuting.
- [ ] Post in discussion boards and reply to classmates.
- [ ] Attend office hours when you need help.

## Track deadlines and progress

- [ ] Check the course site regularly for updates.
- [ ] Submit assignments before each deadline.
- [ ] Confirm each upload went through successfully.
- [ ] Review feedback and grades as they post.
- [ ] Back up your work in the cloud or on a drive.
