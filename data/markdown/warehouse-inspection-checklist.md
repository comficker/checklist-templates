# Warehouse Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting a warehouse for safety and condition.

> Free printable & editable version: [Warehouse Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/warehouse-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Racking and storage

- [ ] Inspect uprights and beams for dents, bends or impact damage. — _Damaged racking must be offloaded and tagged out until repaired by a qualified provider._
- [ ] Confirm load capacity signs are posted and not exceeded.
- [ ] Check beam locks and safety pins are fitted and engaged.
- [ ] Verify baseplates are anchored and uprights are plumb.
- [ ] Confirm loads are stable, within limits and not overhanging.
- [ ] Check shelving and mezzanines for damage and overloading.

## Forklifts and equipment

- [ ] Confirm forklifts have a completed daily pre-use inspection.
- [ ] Check forks, mast and hydraulics for damage and leaks.
- [ ] Verify horn, lights, brakes and seatbelt function.
- [ ] Confirm operators are trained, certified and authorized.
- [ ] Check pedestrian and forklift routes are separated and marked.
- [ ] Verify charging or refueling areas are ventilated and safe.

## Fire safety

- [ ] Confirm fire extinguishers are charged, tagged and accessible.
- [ ] Check sprinklers are unobstructed with adequate clearance below.
- [ ] Verify fire alarms and detectors function on test.
- [ ] Confirm flammable goods are stored in approved areas.
- [ ] Check electrical panels are clear, closed and labeled.
- [ ] Verify no-smoking and fire-safety signage is posted.

## Exits and emergency

- [ ] Confirm all emergency exits are unobstructed and unlocked.
- [ ] Check exit and directional signage is visible and lit.
- [ ] Verify emergency lighting operates during test.
- [ ] Confirm muster points are clear and signposted.
- [ ] Check first aid kits and eyewash stations are stocked.
- [ ] Verify spill kits are available for stored chemicals.

## Housekeeping

- [ ] Confirm aisles and walkways are clear and within marked widths.
- [ ] Check floors for damage, debris, spills or trip hazards.
- [ ] Verify waste and packaging are removed and not piling up.
- [ ] Confirm pallets are stored flat and stacks are stable.
- [ ] Check lighting is adequate throughout the storage areas.
- [ ] Verify nothing is stored blocking equipment or panels.

## Loading dock

- [ ] Inspect dock levelers, plates and edges for damage.
- [ ] Confirm wheel chocks or vehicle restraints are used.
- [ ] Check dock doors operate safely and seal when closed.
- [ ] Verify dock edges and drop-offs are guarded or marked.
- [ ] Confirm lighting and signaling at the dock work correctly.
- [ ] Record defects with an owner and due date, then sign and file.
