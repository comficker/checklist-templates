# Tool Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting hand and power tools before use.

> Free printable & editable version: [Tool Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/tool-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Hand tools

- [ ] Inspect handles for cracks, splinters or looseness.
- [ ] Check striking faces for mushrooming or chips.
- [ ] Verify cutting edges are sharp and undamaged.
- [ ] Confirm jaws, pivots and adjustments work smoothly.
- [ ] Check that tools are clean and free of grease or oil.
- [ ] Confirm the right tool is used for the task, not improvised.

## Power tool body

- [ ] Inspect the housing for cracks, melting or impact damage.
- [ ] Check switches and triggers operate and do not stick.
- [ ] Verify bits, blades and accessories are sharp and secure.
- [ ] Confirm air vents are clear of dust and debris.
- [ ] Check for unusual noise, vibration or burning smell on test.
- [ ] Verify the data plate and ratings are legible.

## Cords and plugs

- [ ] Inspect the cord for cuts, fraying or exposed wires. — _Tools with damaged cords must be removed from service, not taped over._
- [ ] Check the plug for bent, missing or damaged pins.
- [ ] Verify the grounding pin is intact on grounded tools.
- [ ] Confirm strain reliefs at the plug and tool are secure.
- [ ] Check extension cords are rated and undamaged.
- [ ] Verify cords are kept clear of water, heat and traffic.

## Guards and safety devices

- [ ] Confirm blade and rotating-part guards are fitted and move freely.
- [ ] Check that guards have not been removed or wedged open.
- [ ] Verify safety triggers, locks and dead-man switches work.
- [ ] Confirm anti-kickback and riving knives are in place where fitted.
- [ ] Check warning labels are present and legible.
- [ ] Verify required PPE such as eye and hearing protection is available.

## Electrical and GFCI

- [ ] Confirm power tools are used with GFCI protection.
- [ ] Test the GFCI device trips and resets correctly.
- [ ] Verify double-insulated tools show the marking and are intact.
- [ ] Check outlets and connections show no signs of overheating.
- [ ] Confirm tools are unplugged before changing bits or blades.
- [ ] Verify damp or wet conditions are controlled before use.

## Pneumatic, cordless and records

- [ ] Inspect air hoses, couplings and fittings for leaks and damage.
- [ ] Confirm air tools have safety clips or whip checks on connections.
- [ ] Check battery packs and chargers for damage or swelling.
- [ ] Verify batteries seat securely and hold a charge.
- [ ] Tag out and remove any defective tool from service.
- [ ] Record defects and replacements, then sign, date and file.
