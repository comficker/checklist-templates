# Menopause and Perimenopause Symptoms Checklist

A printable symptom tracker for perimenopause and menopause to fill in at home and bring to your doctor's appointment.

> Free printable & editable version: [Menopause and Perimenopause Symptoms Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/menopause-symptoms-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Cycle Changes

- [ ] Date of my last period: ____
- [ ] Note if cycles are shorter, longer or skipped — _Write the start date of each period for a few months. A simple list of dates shows patterns your doctor can use._
- [ ] Note if bleeding is heavier, lighter or lasts longer than before
- [ ] Note any spotting or bleeding between periods or after sex

## Hot Flashes and Night Sweats

- [ ] Tick if you have sudden waves of heat in the face, neck or chest
- [ ] Tick if you wake up sweating or need to change bedding or clothes
- [ ] Note any flushing, racing heartbeat or chills that come with them
- [ ] List possible triggers, such as alcohol, caffeine, spicy food or a hot room — _Knowing your triggers gives you something to try changing and gives your doctor useful detail._

## Sleep, Mood and Memory

- [ ] Tick if you have trouble falling asleep or wake up too early
- [ ] Tick if you feel irritable, low, anxious or have mood swings
- [ ] Tick if you have trouble focusing, find it hard to recall words or feel foggy
- [ ] Note low energy or tiredness that rest does not fix

## Vaginal, Bladder and Sexual Health

- [ ] Tick if you have vaginal dryness, itching or burning
- [ ] Tick if sex is painful or you have less interest in sex
- [ ] Tick if you need to pee more often or more urgently, or leak urine
- [ ] Note any repeated urinary tract infections — _These changes are common and treatable, and they tend to continue after menopause rather than fade, so they are worth raising even if they feel awkward._

## Joints, Skin, Hair and Weight

- [ ] Tick if you have new joint aches or stiffness
- [ ] Tick if your skin is drier, itchier or thinner
- [ ] Tick if hair is thinning on your head or growing more on your face
- [ ] Note weight gain, especially around the waist: ____
- [ ] Note headaches, or migraines that changed in pattern

## Symptom Log

- [ ] Week starting: ____
- [ ] Hot flashes per day: ____ / Night sweats per week: ____
- [ ] Nights of poor sleep this week: ____
- [ ] Worst symptom this week: ____ / Severity (0 none to 10 severe): ____
- [ ] How much symptoms affected work, home or relationships (0 to 10): ____ — _Your doctor will ask how symptoms affect your daily life. A number you tracked is easier to share than a memory._

## Questions to Ask Your Doctor

- [ ] Ask whether my symptoms fit perimenopause, menopause or something else
- [ ] Ask whether I need any tests, such as a thyroid check
- [ ] Discuss treatment options such as HRT, including the benefits and risks for me
- [ ] Ask about non-hormone options and lifestyle changes
- [ ] Ask what to do about birth control until periods stop
- [ ] Ask about bone health and heart health after menopause — _Bring a list of all medicines and supplements you take, plus your family history of breast cancer, blood clots, heart disease and osteoporosis._

## When to Get Care Sooner

- [ ] Call your doctor about any bleeding after menopause, even spotting — _Once you have gone 12 months without a period, any bleeding needs to be checked. It is often caused by something minor, but it can be an early sign of cancer._
- [ ] Get care for very heavy bleeding, such as soaking a pad or tampon every hour or two
- [ ] Get care if periods last longer than 7 days or come closer than 21 days apart
- [ ] Talk to your doctor if symptoms start before age 45 — _Periods that stop before age 40 can be a sign of premature ovarian insufficiency, which needs its own care._
- [ ] Get help right away if you have thoughts of harming yourself; call or text 988
