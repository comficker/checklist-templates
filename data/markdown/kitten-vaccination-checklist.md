# Kitten Vaccination Checklist

A checklist to help new kitten owners follow the typical vaccine series, track each shot, and stay on schedule with their veterinarian.

> Free printable & editable version: [Kitten Vaccination Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/kitten-vaccination-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Before the First Visit

- [ ] Confirm your kitten's age and any shelter records
- [ ] Gather paperwork on shots already given
- [ ] Note your kitten's weight and overall health
- [ ] Write down questions about diet, parasites, and litter habits
- [ ] Book the first vet visit soon after bringing kitten home — _Ask your vet to set the right start date for your kitten_

## Around 6 to 8 Weeks

- [ ] First combination vaccine for feline viruses as advised
- [ ] General health and weight check with your vet
- [ ] Discuss a deworming and parasite plan
- [ ] Ask about a feline leukemia test and prevention
- [ ] Record the date and set the next appointment — _Timing varies by region and vet, so confirm your kitten's schedule_

## Around 10 to 12 Weeks

- [ ] Second combination booster as recommended
- [ ] Feline leukemia vaccine if outdoor access is likely
- [ ] Continue the deworming or parasite plan
- [ ] Review socialization, handling, and litter training
- [ ] Log the visit and confirm the next due date

## Around 14 to 16+ Weeks

- [ ] Final combination booster of the kitten series
- [ ] Rabies vaccine where required by law or your vet
- [ ] Confirm when adult boosters will next be due
- [ ] Discuss spay or neuter timing if relevant
- [ ] Ask when outdoor access is safe if planned — _Vets differ on exact timing, so follow your vet's guidance_

## After Each Shot

- [ ] Watch for mild soreness, tiredness, or low appetite
- [ ] Note any swelling, vomiting, or distress to report
- [ ] Give your kitten a calm, quiet place to rest
- [ ] Update the record and set the next reminder
- [ ] Keep a copy ready for boarding or travel — _Contact your vet promptly if you see a strong reaction_

## Records & Next Steps

- [ ] Keep a running log of each vaccine and date
- [ ] Save batch numbers and the clinic that gave each shot
- [ ] Register and confirm your kitten's microchip details
- [ ] Plan the first adult booster visit
- [ ] Store a digital backup of the full kitten record

## Important

- [ ] This checklist is general information for owners, not veterinary advice; kitten vaccine schedules vary by region and vet, so always consult a licensed veterinarian — _For any reaction or emergency, contact your vet or an emergency animal hospital immediately_
