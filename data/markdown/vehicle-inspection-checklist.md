# Vehicle Inspection Checklist

A pre-trip inspection checklist for vehicles and fleets.

> Free printable & editable version: [Vehicle Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/vehicle-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Tires and wheels

- [ ] Check tire tread depth meets the legal minimum.
- [ ] Inspect tires for cuts, bulges, cracks or embedded objects.
- [ ] Verify tire pressure matches the recommended setting.
- [ ] Confirm wheel nuts are present and tight.
- [ ] Check the spare tire, jack and tools are present where required.
- [ ] Inspect rims for cracks or damage.

## Lights and signals

- [ ] Confirm headlights, high beams and fog lights work.
- [ ] Check brake lights illuminate when the pedal is pressed.
- [ ] Verify turn signals and hazard lights flash correctly.
- [ ] Confirm reverse lights work when in reverse.
- [ ] Check marker, clearance and license plate lights.
- [ ] Verify all lenses are intact and clean.

## Brakes and steering

- [ ] Confirm the brake pedal is firm with normal travel.
- [ ] Check the parking brake holds the vehicle. — _Any brake or steering fault makes the vehicle unsafe to drive until repaired._
- [ ] Verify steering responds smoothly with no excess play.
- [ ] Listen for unusual noise when braking or steering.
- [ ] On air-brake vehicles, check pressure build-up and warnings.
- [ ] Confirm no warning lights remain on for brakes or ABS.

## Fluids and engine

- [ ] Check engine oil level and condition.
- [ ] Verify coolant level when the engine is cool.
- [ ] Check brake and power steering fluid levels.
- [ ] Confirm windshield washer fluid is topped up.
- [ ] Inspect under the vehicle for leaks or drips.
- [ ] Check belts and hoses for wear or looseness.

## Mirrors and cab

- [ ] Confirm mirrors are clean, intact and adjusted.
- [ ] Check the windshield and wipers are clear and functional.
- [ ] Verify the horn works.
- [ ] Confirm seatbelts latch and retract for all seats.
- [ ] Check dashboard warning lights clear after startup.
- [ ] Verify the defroster and ventilation operate.

## Documents and safety

- [ ] Confirm registration and insurance documents are present and current.
- [ ] Check the driver's license is valid for the vehicle class.
- [ ] Verify the first aid kit and warning triangles where required.
- [ ] Confirm the fire extinguisher is charged where fitted.
- [ ] Record any defects and report them before driving.
- [ ] Sign, date and file the completed pre-trip inspection.
