# Visa Checklist

A visa checklist for applying for a travel visa, covering research, required documents, the application, appointments, and follow-up.

> Free printable & editable version: [Visa Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/visa-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Research Requirements

- [ ] Confirm whether you need a visa for your destination
- [ ] Identify the correct visa type for your trip
- [ ] Read the destination's official requirements
- [ ] Check the processing time against your travel dates
- [ ] Verify rules with the destination's embassy — _Visa rules vary by country and change often_

## Prepare Required Documents

- [ ] Passport valid for the required period
- [ ] Photos meeting the visa specifications
- [ ] Proof of funds, such as bank statements
- [ ] Travel bookings and accommodation details
- [ ] Any invitation letters or supporting documents
- [ ] Proof of employment, study, or ties as needed

## Complete the Application

- [ ] Fill in the official application form accurately
- [ ] Match details exactly to your passport
- [ ] Attach all required documents in the right format
- [ ] Review for errors before submitting
- [ ] Keep a copy of the completed application

## Appointment and Fees

- [ ] Confirm the current visa fee and payment method
- [ ] Book any required appointment or interview
- [ ] Prepare for biometrics if needed
- [ ] Bring originals and copies to your appointment
- [ ] Note the location and time carefully

## Submit and Follow Up

- [ ] Submit your application by the required method
- [ ] Keep your receipt and tracking reference
- [ ] Monitor the application status
- [ ] Respond quickly to any requests for more documents
- [ ] Check visa details for errors once issued
- [ ] Confirm current rules before relying on this list — _Visa requirements, documents, fees, and timelines vary by country and change; always verify with the destination's embassy or official immigration authority_
