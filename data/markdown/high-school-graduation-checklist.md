# High School Graduation Checklist

A senior-year checklist covering graduation requirements, the ceremony and next-step planning.

> Free printable & editable version: [High School Graduation Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/high-school-graduation-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Confirm graduation requirements

- [ ] Meet with your counselor to review your transcript. — _Do this early so there's time to fix any credit gaps._
- [ ] Confirm you've met all required credits and courses.
- [ ] Check any required exams or assessments are passed.
- [ ] Verify community service or other requirements are met.
- [ ] Confirm your GPA and class standing.
- [ ] Resolve any incomplete or failing grades.

## Plan your next step

- [ ] Decide on college, work, trade, military or a gap year.
- [ ] Complete the applications for your chosen path.
- [ ] Apply for financial aid and scholarships if relevant.
- [ ] Meet with advisors or recruiters for guidance.
- [ ] Track acceptance, enrollment or start-date deadlines.
- [ ] Make a final decision and notify the school you choose.

## Senior-year tasks

- [ ] Keep your grades up through final transcripts.
- [ ] Take senior portraits by the school's deadline.
- [ ] Participate in senior events and activities.
- [ ] Update your resume with awards and activities.
- [ ] Ask for any final letters of recommendation.
- [ ] Stay on top of every deadline on your calendar.

## Ceremony preparation

- [ ] Order your cap, gown and any honor cords early.
- [ ] Order class ring or announcements if you want them.
- [ ] Confirm the ceremony date, time and location.
- [ ] Find out how many guest tickets you receive.
- [ ] Confirm your name spelling for the diploma.
- [ ] Attend graduation rehearsal if one is held.

## Clear obligations

- [ ] Pay any outstanding fees or fines.
- [ ] Return textbooks, equipment and library books.
- [ ] Clean out your locker and personal spaces.
- [ ] Request official final transcripts for your next step.
- [ ] Update your contact and mailing information.
- [ ] Confirm you're cleared to receive your diploma.

## Celebrate and transition

- [ ] Plan or attend the graduation celebration.
- [ ] Take family and friend photos at the ceremony.
- [ ] Thank teachers, coaches and mentors who helped you.
- [ ] Save copies of your diploma and key records.
- [ ] Set goals for the summer before your next step.
- [ ] Stay connected with friends after graduation.
