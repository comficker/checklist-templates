# Homeschool Supplies Checklist

A complete shopping and stocking list of supplies and materials for homeschooling.

> Free printable & editable version: [Homeschool Supplies Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/homeschool-supplies-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Curriculum and books

- [ ] Core curriculum for each subject and grade level.
- [ ] Workbooks and consumable lesson materials.
- [ ] Reading books at each child's level.
- [ ] Reference books such as a dictionary and atlas.
- [ ] Teacher guides and answer keys.
- [ ] A library card and list of free online resources.

## Writing and paper

- [ ] Pencils, pens, erasers and a sharpener.
- [ ] Notebooks, composition books and loose-leaf paper.
- [ ] Highlighters, colored pencils and markers.
- [ ] Construction and chart paper for projects.
- [ ] Index cards and sticky notes.
- [ ] A whiteboard with dry-erase markers. — _Great for modeling math and quick lessons._

## Hands-on learning materials

- [ ] Math manipulatives such as counters and blocks.
- [ ] A science kit and basic experiment supplies.
- [ ] Art materials: paint, glue, scissors and clay.
- [ ] Measuring tools, rulers and a calculator.
- [ ] Maps, globes and timeline materials.
- [ ] Building sets, puzzles and educational games.

## Organization and storage

- [ ] Binders, folders and dividers for each subject.
- [ ] Labeled bins and baskets for supplies.
- [ ] A separate storage spot for each child's work.
- [ ] A filing system for records and portfolios.
- [ ] A planner or calendar for the homeschool schedule.
- [ ] A bookshelf or cart for curriculum and supplies.

## Technology and tools

- [ ] A computer or tablet for lessons and research.
- [ ] Reliable internet access for online curriculum.
- [ ] A printer with paper and spare ink.
- [ ] Headphones for focused or online work.
- [ ] Educational apps and subscription logins.
- [ ] A backup or cloud storage for files and records.

## Extras and enrichment

- [ ] Art and craft supplies for creative projects.
- [ ] Musical instruments or audio resources.
- [ ] Materials for physical activity and outdoor play.
- [ ] A field-trip bag with clipboard and supplies.
- [ ] Rewards, stickers or a progress chart.
- [ ] Seasonal and holiday learning materials.
