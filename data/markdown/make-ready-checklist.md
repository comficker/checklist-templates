# Make Ready Checklist

A unit turnover checklist for property managers and maintenance techs getting a vacant apartment or rental home ready for the next tenant.

> Free printable & editable version: [Make Ready Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/make-ready-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Move-Out Inspection

- [ ] Collect all keys, fobs, remotes and parking passes from the old tenant
- [ ] Photograph and video every room, wall, floor and appliance — _Turn on time stamps and compare against the move-in photos for the same unit._
- [ ] Compare the unit to the move-in condition report
- [ ] List damage beyond normal wear and tear for the deposit statement
- [ ] Write the scope of work and order parts, flooring and appliances

## Safety and Systems

- [ ] Test every smoke alarm and replace batteries or expired units — _Most smoke alarms should be replaced 10 years after the date printed on the back._
- [ ] Test carbon monoxide alarms where there are gas appliances or an attached garage
- [ ] Replace the HVAC filter and test heating and cooling
- [ ] Check under sinks, around toilets and at the water heater for leaks
- [ ] Test GFCI outlets in the kitchen, bathrooms and outdoors — _Press TEST, confirm power cuts off, then press RESET._
- [ ] Rekey or change all exterior door locks — _Rekey mailbox locks too, if you control them._

## Kitchen and Appliances

- [ ] Run the oven, cooktop burners and range hood fan
- [ ] Check the fridge and freezer hold temperature and the seals close tight
- [ ] Run the dishwasher and garbage disposal through a full cycle
- [ ] Check cabinet doors, drawers and hinges, and fix any that sag

## Bathrooms

- [ ] Flush each toilet and check it does not run or rock
- [ ] Check caulk around tubs, showers and sinks, and recaulk if moldy or cracked
- [ ] Test the exhaust fan and check drains run freely

## Paint and Repairs

- [ ] Patch holes from nails, anchors and wall mounts
- [ ] Touch up or repaint walls, trim and doors — _In homes built before 1978, use an EPA lead-safe certified firm for paint work that disturbs more than 6 sq ft per room inside._
- [ ] Replace burnt-out bulbs and broken switch or outlet covers
- [ ] Fix window screens, blinds, door stops and closet doors
- [ ] Repair or replace damaged flooring and carpet

## Deep Clean and Pest Check

- [ ] Clean inside the oven, fridge, microwave, cabinets and drawers
- [ ] Scrub tubs, showers, toilets, tile and grout
- [ ] Wipe baseboards, doors, light fixtures, vents and windowsills
- [ ] Shampoo or replace carpet and mop hard floors
- [ ] Look for droppings, roaches, ants and bed bug signs and treat if found — _Check behind the fridge and stove and under the sinks, where pests hide first._

## Final Walk-Through and Keys

- [ ] Walk every room with the checklist and confirm all work is done
- [ ] Test each new key in every lock
- [ ] Set the thermostat and leave lights in a standard setting
- [ ] Keep power and water on in the owner's name until the new lease starts
- [ ] Take final photos to use for the next move-in condition report
