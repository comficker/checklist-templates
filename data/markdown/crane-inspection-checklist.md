# Crane Inspection Checklist

A pre-use inspection checklist for cranes.

> Free printable & editable version: [Crane Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/crane-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Rigging and hooks

- [ ] Inspect slings for cuts, fraying, kinks and broken wires.
- [ ] Check shackles, hooks and fittings for wear, cracks and deformation. — _Remove from service any hook with cracks, excessive throat opening or twist._
- [ ] Verify hook safety latches close and function.
- [ ] Confirm rigging is rated for the load and within its capacity.
- [ ] Check tags and certifications on rigging are current.
- [ ] Verify the load is correctly secured and balanced before lifting.

## Wire rope and hoist

- [ ] Inspect wire rope for broken wires, kinks, birdcaging and corrosion.
- [ ] Check rope spooling is even on the drum with no overlap.
- [ ] Verify the rope is correctly seated in sheaves.
- [ ] Inspect sheaves and drums for wear and grooving.
- [ ] Confirm rope end connections are secure and undamaged.
- [ ] Check lubrication of the rope and reeving.

## Controls and instruments

- [ ] Confirm all controls operate smoothly and return to neutral.
- [ ] Check the load moment indicator and readouts function.
- [ ] Verify gauges, warning lights and alarms read normally.
- [ ] Test hoist, swing, boom and travel functions.
- [ ] Confirm brakes hold and operate correctly.
- [ ] Check the cab, mirrors and visibility are clear.

## Load chart and capacity

- [ ] Confirm the correct load chart for the configuration is in the cab.
- [ ] Verify the load weight is known and within capacity.
- [ ] Check boom length, radius and angle against the chart.
- [ ] Confirm counterweight matches the configuration.
- [ ] Verify the lift plan accounts for wind and ground conditions.
- [ ] Confirm clearances from power lines and obstructions.

## Outriggers and stability

- [ ] Confirm outriggers are fully extended and pinned per the chart.
- [ ] Check outrigger pads and cribbing are rated and on firm ground.
- [ ] Verify the crane is level within tolerance.
- [ ] Confirm tires are off the ground when fully on outriggers.
- [ ] Check the ground bearing capacity supports the loads.
- [ ] Verify the swing area is clear and barricaded.

## Safety devices and records

- [ ] Test the anti-two-block device and warning.
- [ ] Confirm limit switches and overload protection function.
- [ ] Check the horn, alarms and signaling work.
- [ ] Verify the fire extinguisher is charged and mounted.
- [ ] Confirm operator certification and inspection records are current.
- [ ] Record defects, take unsafe cranes out of service, then sign and file.
