# Essay Checklist

An essay checklist that helps students plan, write and polish a clear, well-structured essay.

> Free printable & editable version: [Essay Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/essay-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Understand the prompt

- [ ] Read the prompt twice and underline the key task words. — _Words like analyze, compare or argue tell you what to do._
- [ ] Note the required length, format and due date.
- [ ] Identify the type of essay, such as argumentative or analytical.
- [ ] List any required sources or texts you must reference.
- [ ] Ask your teacher to clarify anything you are unsure about.

## Build your thesis

- [ ] Decide your main point or position on the topic.
- [ ] Write a one-sentence thesis that is specific and arguable.
- [ ] Check that the thesis answers the prompt directly.
- [ ] Make sure you can support it with at least three points.
- [ ] Keep the thesis in view while you plan and write.

## Plan the structure

- [ ] Sketch a quick outline: introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion.
- [ ] Assign one main idea to each body paragraph.
- [ ] Order the paragraphs so the argument builds logically.
- [ ] Note the evidence you will use in each paragraph.
- [ ] Plan smooth transitions between sections.

## Write the introduction

- [ ] Open with a hook that draws the reader in.
- [ ] Give brief context the reader needs to follow your argument.
- [ ] End the introduction with your thesis statement.
- [ ] Keep it concise and avoid summarizing the whole essay.

## Develop the body

- [ ] Start each paragraph with a clear topic sentence.
- [ ] Back every claim with specific evidence or examples.
- [ ] Explain how each piece of evidence supports your thesis.
- [ ] Cite quotes and sources where required.
- [ ] Use transitions to link ideas across paragraphs.
- [ ] Stay on topic and cut anything that doesn't serve the argument.

## Write the conclusion

- [ ] Restate the thesis in fresh wording.
- [ ] Summarize the main points without repeating them word for word.
- [ ] End with a final thought, implication or call to reflection.
- [ ] Avoid introducing brand-new arguments here.

## Proofread and submit

- [ ] Read the essay aloud to catch awkward phrasing.
- [ ] Fix spelling, grammar and punctuation errors.
- [ ] Check that every paragraph supports the thesis.
- [ ] Confirm formatting, citations and word count meet the requirements.
- [ ] Submit before the deadline and keep a saved copy.
