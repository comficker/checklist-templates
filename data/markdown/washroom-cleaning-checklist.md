# Washroom Cleaning Checklist

A washroom and restroom cleaning checklist for offices, schools, shops and public facilities, with an hourly check log and weekly deep-clean tasks.

> Free printable & editable version: [Washroom Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/washroom-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Before You Start

- [ ] Put up a wet-floor or cleaning-in-progress sign at the entrance
- [ ] Put on gloves and any eye protection the product label requires
- [ ] Use separate color-coded cloths for toilets and for sinks and surfaces — _A common scheme is red for toilets and urinals, yellow for sinks and other washroom surfaces._
- [ ] Check the disinfectant's contact time on the label before you begin — _The surface must stay wet for the full contact time or the disinfectant won't work._
- [ ] Open a window or turn on the extractor fan for ventilation

## Restock and Waste

- [ ] Refill toilet paper and keep a spare roll in every cubicle
- [ ] Refill soap and hand sanitizer dispensers
- [ ] Refill paper towels or check that hand dryers work
- [ ] Empty bins and sanitary bins and fit new liners — _Sanitary bins are usually emptied by a specialist contractor; check the service schedule rather than emptying them by hand._
- [ ] Report any broken dispenser, lock or hand dryer

## High-Touch Points

- [ ] Disinfect door handles, push plates and cubicle locks
- [ ] Disinfect flush handles, taps and dispenser buttons
- [ ] Wipe light switches and baby-changing tables

## Toilets and Urinals

- [ ] Apply toilet cleaner under the rim and leave it to work
- [ ] Clean the seat, hinges, lid and outside of the bowl
- [ ] Scrub the bowl and under the rim, then flush
- [ ] Clean urinals, including the bowl, wall behind and floor in front
- [ ] Replace urinal screens or blocks when worn down
- [ ] Wipe cubicle walls and partitions at hand height

## Sinks, Counters and Mirrors

- [ ] Clean sinks, taps and the area around the overflow
- [ ] Wipe counters and the wall splashback dry
- [ ] Clean mirrors with glass cleaner for a streak-free finish
- [ ] Check the sinks drain freely

## Floors and Drains

- [ ] Sweep or dust-mop the floor first
- [ ] Mop from the far corner toward the exit with clean solution
- [ ] Pay attention to the floor around toilet bases and urinals — _Urine splashes around urinals are a common cause of lingering odor._
- [ ] Pour water into floor drains to keep the trap from drying out
- [ ] Remove the wet-floor sign only when the floor is dry

## Hourly Check Log

- [ ] Time checked: ____
- [ ] Checked by (initials): ____
- [ ] Supplies restocked: Yes / No
- [ ] Floor dry and bins below half full: Yes / No
- [ ] Issues reported: ____

## Weekly Deep Clean

- [ ] Descale toilets, urinals and taps
- [ ] Scrub grout and floor edges
- [ ] Wash walls, cubicle doors and partitions top to bottom
- [ ] Dust vents, extractor fan covers and high ledges
- [ ] Clean and disinfect bins inside and out
