# Hotel Housekeeping Checklist

A room attendant's housekeeping checklist for cleaning a hotel, guesthouse or B&B room from check-out to guest-ready.

> Free printable & editable version: [Hotel Housekeeping Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/hotel-housekeeping-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Cart and Entry

- [ ] Stock the cart with linen, towels, amenities and cleaning supplies
- [ ] Knock, announce "Housekeeping" and wait before entering
- [ ] Prop the door open and park the cart across the doorway
- [ ] Open curtains and windows, and switch on all lights to check bulbs

## Strip and Clear

- [ ] Check the room for guest property before removing anything — _Look under the bed, in drawers, the safe, the wardrobe and behind the bathroom door._
- [ ] Strip the bed and check the mattress protector for stains
- [ ] Remove used towels, bath mat and robes
- [ ] Empty all bins and replace liners
- [ ] Remove used cups and glasses for washing or replacement

## Bathroom

- [ ] Apply cleaner to the toilet, shower, bath and sink and leave it to work
- [ ] Clean the shower, glass screen, tiles and tub
- [ ] Clean the toilet inside and out, including the base and behind the seat
- [ ] Clean the sink, counter, taps and mirror
- [ ] Wipe down and polish the chrome fittings
- [ ] Check for hair in the drain, shower and floor corners — _Hair is the most common complaint in guest reviews of bathroom cleanliness._
- [ ] Mop the floor and hang fresh towels and bath mat

## Bed Making

- [ ] Fit a fresh bottom sheet or check the mattress protector is clean
- [ ] Put on the top sheet or duvet with fresh cover, smooth and square
- [ ] Put fresh cases on all pillows and arrange them to the property standard
- [ ] Place the bed runner or throw, if used

## Dust and Surfaces

- [ ] Dust from high to low: lights, frames, headboard, shelves
- [ ] Wipe the desk, bedside tables and wardrobe shelves
- [ ] Disinfect high-touch items: remote, phone, light switches, door handles
- [ ] Clean the mirror and any glass surfaces
- [ ] Check that the TV, lamps, kettle, safe and air conditioning work

## Restock Amenities

- [ ] Replace toiletries, toilet paper and tissues
- [ ] Restock tea, coffee, sugar, cups and water
- [ ] Refill the minibar and record what was used, if applicable
- [ ] Replace the notepad, pen and guest information
- [ ] Check hangers, the iron and the hairdryer are in place

## Floors and Final Inspection

- [ ] Vacuum from the far corner, backing out toward the door
- [ ] Look at the room from the doorway as a guest would
- [ ] Set curtains, lights and air conditioning to the property standard
- [ ] Log lost property with room number, date and description
- [ ] Report maintenance faults such as bulbs, leaks or broken fittings
- [ ] Lock the door and mark the room clean in the system
