# Spring Cleaning Checklist

An annual spring cleaning checklist to declutter, deep clean, and refresh your whole home.

> Free printable & editable version: [Spring Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/spring-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Declutter and Organize

- [ ] Sort each room into keep, donate, sell, and discard piles — _Bag donations immediately and put them in the car so they actually leave._
- [ ] Clear out expired food from the pantry, fridge, and freezer
- [ ] Purge expired medicines and toiletries from cabinets
- [ ] Sort through closets and set aside clothes you no longer wear
- [ ] Organize the garage, shed, or storage area and discard broken items
- [ ] Shred old paperwork and tidy filing

## Whole-Home Deep Clean

- [ ] Wash baseboards, door frames, and trim throughout the house
- [ ] Dust ceiling corners, fans, vents, and light fixtures
- [ ] Wipe down walls and spot-clean scuffs and marks
- [ ] Deep clean the oven, fridge, and behind large appliances
- [ ] Scrub bathroom grout, re-caulk if needed, and descale fixtures
- [ ] Vacuum and steam-clean carpets, rugs, and upholstery

## Windows and Letting Light In

- [ ] Wash interior and exterior windows until streak-free
- [ ] Vacuum and wipe window tracks, sills, and frames
- [ ] Launder or dry-clean curtains and dust blinds slat by slat
- [ ] Wash glass on storm and patio doors, including the tracks
- [ ] Clean screens by rinsing them outside and letting them dry

## Soft Furnishings and Bedrooms

- [ ] Wash or dry-clean comforters, duvets, and mattress protectors
- [ ] Rotate or flip mattresses and vacuum both sides
- [ ] Launder throw pillows, blankets, and cushion covers
- [ ] Wash pillows and replace any that are flat or stained
- [ ] Swap out winter bedding for lighter spring linens
- [ ] Wipe down and reorganize dresser drawers and closet shelves

## Kitchen Refresh

- [ ] Empty, wipe, and reline all cabinets and drawers
- [ ] Clean and reorganize the pantry, grouping like items
- [ ] Descale the kettle, coffee maker, and dishwasher
- [ ] Degrease the range hood, backsplash, and cabinet fronts
- [ ] Polish stainless steel and small appliances
- [ ] Deep clean the sink, disposal, and drain

## Outdoor and Seasonal

- [ ] Sweep and power-wash the patio, deck, or balcony
- [ ] Clean out gutters and check downspouts for blockages — _Wear gloves and use a stable ladder with a spotter for safety._
- [ ] Wash outdoor furniture and refresh cushions
- [ ] Sweep the garage and clear winter debris from walkways
- [ ] Wipe down the front door, porch, and outdoor light fixtures
- [ ] Tidy garden beds and store away winter tools
