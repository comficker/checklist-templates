# Building Inspection Checklist

A checklist for inspecting a building's overall condition.

> Free printable & editable version: [Building Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/building-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Structure and foundation

- [ ] Inspect the foundation for cracks, movement or water intrusion.
- [ ] Check walls and ceilings for cracks, bowing or staining.
- [ ] Verify load-bearing elements show no sagging or deflection. — _Suspected structural movement requires assessment by a qualified engineer._
- [ ] Inspect floors for unevenness, soft spots or excessive deflection.
- [ ] Check for signs of damp, rot or termite or pest damage.
- [ ] Confirm expansion joints and supports are intact.

## Roof and exterior

- [ ] Inspect the roof covering for missing, cracked or lifted materials.
- [ ] Check flashing, gutters and downpipes for damage and blockages.
- [ ] Verify drainage directs water away from the building.
- [ ] Inspect exterior walls, cladding and render for cracks or deterioration.
- [ ] Check windows and doors seal properly and operate freely.
- [ ] Inspect parking, paths and steps for trip hazards and damage.

## Electrical systems

- [ ] Check the main panel for proper labeling, no scorching and a clear front.
- [ ] Verify breakers and GFCI/RCD protection operate correctly.
- [ ] Inspect outlets, switches and fixtures for damage or overheating.
- [ ] Confirm no exposed, frayed or temporary wiring is in use.
- [ ] Check emergency and exit lighting functions on test.
- [ ] Verify any electrical work appears compliant and certified.

## Plumbing and HVAC

- [ ] Check for leaks, drips and water staining at fixtures and pipes.
- [ ] Verify hot and cold water flow and adequate drainage.
- [ ] Inspect the water heater for leaks, corrosion and a safety valve.
- [ ] Confirm HVAC units run, with clean filters and clear vents.
- [ ] Check for unusual noise, odor or poor airflow in the system.
- [ ] Verify backflow prevention and shutoff valves are accessible.

## Fire and life safety

- [ ] Confirm smoke and fire detectors are present and test correctly.
- [ ] Check fire extinguishers are charged, tagged and accessible.
- [ ] Verify fire exits are unobstructed and doors open freely.
- [ ] Confirm exit and emergency signage is visible and lit.
- [ ] Check sprinkler and alarm systems show current service records.
- [ ] Verify fire-rated doors and barriers are intact and self-closing.

## Interior and documentation

- [ ] Inspect interior finishes for damage, mold or water staining.
- [ ] Check stairs, handrails and balustrades are secure and code-height.
- [ ] Verify accessibility features and clearances are maintained.
- [ ] Confirm ventilation is adequate in bathrooms and kitchens.
- [ ] Record each defect with severity, location and recommended action.
- [ ] Sign, date and file the completed inspection report.
