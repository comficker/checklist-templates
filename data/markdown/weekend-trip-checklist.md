# Weekend Trip Checklist

A light-packing weekend getaway checklist covering bookings, a carry-on capsule wardrobe, toiletries, tech and pre-departure tasks.

> Free printable & editable version: [Weekend Trip Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/weekend-trip-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Before You Go

- [ ] Confirm travel times and check-in details
- [ ] Save accommodation address and booking confirmation
- [ ] Check the weather at your destination
- [ ] Make a quick plan for meals or activities
- [ ] Set an out-of-office reply if needed
- [ ] Arrange care for pets or plants

## Documents and Money

- [ ] ID or passport
- [ ] Booking confirmations and tickets
- [ ] Debit or credit card and some cash
- [ ] Insurance card and emergency contact details

## Clothing (Keep It Light)

- [ ] Two to three versatile outfits — _Pick a single color palette so everything mixes and matches._
- [ ] One pair of comfortable walking shoes
- [ ] A light jacket or layer for evenings
- [ ] Underwear and socks for each day plus one spare
- [ ] Sleepwear
- [ ] Swimwear if your stay has a pool or beach

## Toiletries and Personal Care

- [ ] Toothbrush and travel-sized toothpaste
- [ ] Travel-sized shampoo, soap, and deodorant
- [ ] Skincare and any makeup you use daily
- [ ] Daily medications and a small first-aid kit
- [ ] Sunscreen and lip balm
- [ ] Hairbrush and basic grooming items

## Tech and Essentials

- [ ] Phone and charging cable
- [ ] Power bank for the day out
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Sunglasses
- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] A book or downloaded entertainment for the journey

## Last-Minute Checks

- [ ] Charge your phone and devices fully
- [ ] Empty the fridge of anything that will spoil
- [ ] Lock windows and doors
- [ ] Adjust the thermostat and unplug small appliances
- [ ] Double-check you have keys, wallet, and phone
