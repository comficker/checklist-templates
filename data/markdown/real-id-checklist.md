# REAL ID Checklist

The documents to gather before a REAL ID appointment, grouped the way the DMV asks for them.

> Free printable & editable version: [REAL ID Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/real-id-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Proof of identity (bring one)

- [ ] Certified birth certificate with a raised or printed seal — _A hospital souvenir certificate is not accepted._
- [ ] Valid unexpired US passport or passport card
- [ ] Certificate of Naturalization or Citizenship
- [ ] Permanent Resident Card, or an unexpired foreign passport with a valid visa
- [ ] Consular Report of Birth Abroad

## Proof of Social Security number (bring one)

- [ ] Social Security card, unlaminated and signed
- [ ] W-2 form showing your full SSN
- [ ] SSA-1099 or non-SSA-1099 form
- [ ] Pay stub showing your name and full SSN — _Many offices reject stubs that mask the number to the last four digits._

## Proof of address (bring two, different sources)

- [ ] Utility bill dated within the last 60-90 days
- [ ] Bank or credit card statement with your name and street address
- [ ] Mortgage statement, deed, or signed rental lease
- [ ] Vehicle registration or insurance card
- [ ] Current state ID or driver's licence showing the same address
- [ ] Official mail from a federal, state or local agency
- [ ] Check both documents show the SAME address, spelled the same way — _A unit number on one and not the other is enough to be turned away._
- [ ] A PO box will not work as a residential address

## Name-change papers (only if your name differs)

- [ ] Certified marriage certificate
- [ ] Divorce decree showing the name restoration
- [ ] Court order for a legal name change
- [ ] Line up every change in order, from the name on your birth certificate to the name you use now — _If you have married twice, the chain needs both certificates. A gap breaks the application._

## Before you leave the house

- [ ] Check your state DMV page for extra documents it requires
- [ ] Book an appointment if your state offers one
- [ ] Complete the application form online in advance if available
- [ ] Confirm accepted payment methods for the fee — _Some offices still refuse credit cards._
- [ ] Pack originals or certified copies, not photocopies
- [ ] Put everything in one folder in the order above
