# Equipment Inspection Checklist

A general checklist for inspecting equipment before use.

> Free printable & editable version: [Equipment Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/equipment-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## General condition

- [ ] Inspect the body and frame for cracks, dents or corrosion.
- [ ] Check fasteners, bolts and mounts are tight and secure.
- [ ] Verify the data plate and rating labels are present and legible.
- [ ] Confirm no missing or damaged components or panels.
- [ ] Check moving parts for wear, play or misalignment.
- [ ] Verify the equipment is clean and free of debris buildup.

## Safety devices

- [ ] Confirm guards and shields are fitted and not bypassed. — _Never operate equipment with a missing guard or defeated safety device._
- [ ] Check emergency stop functions and resets correctly.
- [ ] Verify interlocks and safety switches operate as intended.
- [ ] Confirm warning labels and safety signage are present and legible.
- [ ] Check lockout/tagout points are accessible and functional.
- [ ] Verify any required guarding sensors are clean and working.

## Function and controls

- [ ] Confirm controls, switches and buttons operate smoothly.
- [ ] Check the equipment starts, runs and stops normally.
- [ ] Verify gauges, indicators and displays read correctly.
- [ ] Listen for unusual noise, vibration or grinding in operation.
- [ ] Confirm speed, pressure or output stays within rated limits.
- [ ] Check that any safety functions activate as designed.

## Fluids and leaks

- [ ] Check fluid levels including oil, coolant and hydraulic fluid.
- [ ] Inspect for leaks, drips or stains under and around the equipment.
- [ ] Verify hoses, lines and fittings are sound and not chafed.
- [ ] Confirm seals and gaskets show no weeping or damage.
- [ ] Check lubrication points are greased per the schedule.
- [ ] Verify filters are clean or replaced as required.

## Electrical and power

- [ ] Inspect cords, plugs and cables for damage or fraying.
- [ ] Confirm grounding and GFCI protection are in place where required.
- [ ] Check batteries are charged, secured and free of corrosion.
- [ ] Verify connections are tight and free of overheating signs.
- [ ] Confirm guards are in place over electrical components.
- [ ] Check that the equipment isolates and de-energizes correctly.

## Records and follow-up

- [ ] Confirm the equipment has a current inspection and service record.
- [ ] Verify the last maintenance was completed on schedule.
- [ ] Check certifications or calibration are in date where required.
- [ ] Record any defect with severity and a recommended action.
- [ ] Tag out and report any equipment that fails inspection.
- [ ] Sign, date and file the completed inspection record.
