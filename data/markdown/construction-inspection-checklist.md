# Construction Inspection Checklist

A construction inspection checklist for verifying workmanship and quality at each building stage.

> Free printable & editable version: [Construction Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/construction-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Site and foundation

- [ ] Verify site grading, drainage and erosion controls.
- [ ] Check excavation depth, soil conditions and compaction.
- [ ] Confirm footing dimensions, rebar size and placement.
- [ ] Inspect formwork alignment, bracing and dimensions.
- [ ] Verify foundation waterproofing and damp-proofing.
- [ ] Check anchor bolts and embedded items before the pour. — _Confirm a code-required foundation inspection is passed before backfilling._

## Structural framing

- [ ] Verify framing members match the structural drawings.
- [ ] Check stud, joist and rafter spacing and sizing.
- [ ] Confirm headers, beams and load paths are correct.
- [ ] Inspect connections, fasteners and hardware.
- [ ] Check that openings are framed to plan dimensions.
- [ ] Verify the structure is plumb, level and square.

## Mechanical, electrical and plumbing rough-in

- [ ] Confirm electrical rough-in, boxes and wire gauge match plans.
- [ ] Pressure-test and inspect plumbing supply and drain lines.
- [ ] Verify HVAC ductwork routing, sizing and supports.
- [ ] Check that penetrations are properly sealed and fire-stopped.
- [ ] Confirm fixtures and equipment locations match drawings.
- [ ] Inspect for code clearances and accessibility before cover-up.

## Insulation and envelope

- [ ] Verify insulation type and R-value meet specifications.
- [ ] Check vapor barriers and air sealing for continuity.
- [ ] Inspect window and door flashing and weatherproofing.
- [ ] Confirm exterior cladding installation and fastening.
- [ ] Check roofing materials, underlayment and flashing.
- [ ] Verify drainage planes and weep paths are clear.

## Interior finishes

- [ ] Inspect drywall finish, seams and surface quality.
- [ ] Check paint coverage, color and edges.
- [ ] Verify flooring installation, levelness and transitions.
- [ ] Inspect cabinetry, millwork and trim alignment.
- [ ] Confirm fixtures, hardware and accessories are installed correctly.
- [ ] Check tile layout, grout lines and waterproofing.

## Final and systems verification

- [ ] Test electrical outlets, switches and GFCI protection.
- [ ] Confirm plumbing fixtures operate without leaks.
- [ ] Verify HVAC heating, cooling and airflow performance.
- [ ] Check that life-safety and fire systems function correctly.
- [ ] Review the punch list and confirm corrections are complete.
- [ ] Document findings with photos and a signed inspection report.
