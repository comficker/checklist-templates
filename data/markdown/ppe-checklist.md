# PPE Checklist

A check for selecting, using and inspecting personal protective equipment at work.

> Free printable & editable version: [PPE Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/ppe-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Hazard Assessment and Selection

- [ ] Confirm a written PPE hazard assessment has been completed.
- [ ] Match each type of PPE to the specific hazard it must control. — _PPE is the last line of defense after other controls._
- [ ] Verify equipment meets the applicable standard for the hazard.
- [ ] Confirm sizes and options are available for all workers.
- [ ] Check that PPE is provided at no cost where required.

## Head, Eye and Face Protection

- [ ] Confirm hard hats are worn where impact or falling-object hazards exist.
- [ ] Inspect helmets for cracks, dents and expired suspension.
- [ ] Verify safety glasses, goggles or face shields match the hazard.
- [ ] Check eye protection fits and provides side protection. — _Use goggles or shields for splash, dust or high-impact tasks._
- [ ] Confirm welding shields and filters are correct for the work.

## Hearing and Respiratory Protection

- [ ] Confirm hearing protection is used where noise exceeds safe limits.
- [ ] Verify the noise reduction rating suits the exposure level.
- [ ] Confirm respirators match the airborne hazard and concentration.
- [ ] Check that tight-fitting respirators are fit tested and seal checked. — _Facial hair can break a respirator seal and must be managed._
- [ ] Verify respirator filters and cartridges are correct and in date.

## Hand and Foot Protection

- [ ] Select gloves for the hazard: cut, chemical, heat or electrical.
- [ ] Inspect gloves for tears, holes, wear or chemical breakthrough.
- [ ] Confirm safety footwear suits impact, puncture and slip hazards.
- [ ] Check footwear for sole wear, damage and intact protective toes.
- [ ] Verify electrical or chemical footwear is used where required.

## Inspection and Maintenance

- [ ] Inspect all PPE before each use for damage and wear.
- [ ] Remove damaged or expired PPE from service immediately.
- [ ] Clean, disinfect and store PPE per manufacturer instructions. — _Do not share personal PPE without proper cleaning._
- [ ] Confirm replacement stock is available when items wear out.
- [ ] Keep records of issue, inspection and fit testing.

## Training and Use

- [ ] Confirm workers are trained on why, when and how to use each item.
- [ ] Verify workers can put on, adjust and remove PPE correctly.
- [ ] Check that PPE is worn consistently and not removed in hazard areas.
- [ ] Confirm workers know how to report damaged or missing PPE.
- [ ] Verify refresher training is provided as needed.
- [ ] Important: this is general guidance only and does not replace OSHA regulations, formal training, manufacturer instructions or your employer's safety program; confirm requirements with a qualified person.
