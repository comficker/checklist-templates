# Vacation Packing Checklist

A vacation packing checklist for a leisure getaway, covering clothes, beach and pool gear, toiletries, tech, and relaxation essentials.

> Free printable & editable version: [Vacation Packing Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/vacation-packing-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Vacation Clothing

- [ ] Casual tops and t-shirts
- [ ] Shorts, skirts, or light trousers
- [ ] A relaxed dress or evening outfit
- [ ] Underwear and socks for the trip
- [ ] Sleepwear and a light layer for evenings
- [ ] A sweater or jacket for cooler moments — _Pack layers even for warm destinations_

## Beach and Pool

- [ ] Swimwear, plus a spare
- [ ] Beach towel or quick-dry travel towel
- [ ] Cover-up or sarong
- [ ] Flip-flops or sandals
- [ ] Sun hat and sunglasses
- [ ] Goggles or snorkel gear if relevant

## Toiletries and Sun Care

- [ ] Sunscreen and after-sun lotion
- [ ] Toothbrush, toothpaste, and deodorant
- [ ] Shampoo and soap in travel sizes
- [ ] Skincare and lip balm with SPF
- [ ] Insect repellent if needed
- [ ] Medicines and a small first-aid kit

## Tech and Entertainment

- [ ] Phone, charger, and power bank
- [ ] Travel adapter for the destination
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Camera for photos
- [ ] A book, e-reader, or downloaded shows
- [ ] Waterproof phone pouch for the beach

## Documents and Money

- [ ] Passport or ID and any visas
- [ ] Booking confirmations, printed and digital
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] Cash, cards, and a travel card
- [ ] Copies of key documents stored separately

## Relaxation Extras

- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] Day bag for excursions
- [ ] Snacks for travel days
- [ ] Neck pillow and eye mask
- [ ] Packing cubes and a laundry bag
- [ ] Wipes, tissues, and hand sanitizer
