# Forklift Inspection Checklist

A daily pre-operation inspection checklist for forklifts.

> Free printable & editable version: [Forklift Inspection Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/forklift-inspection-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Visual checks (engine off)

- [ ] Check for fluid leaks or puddles under the forklift.
- [ ] Inspect forks for cracks, bends, wear and a sound heel.
- [ ] Verify the load backrest and carriage are secure and undamaged.
- [ ] Check the mast, chains and rollers for wear and proper lubrication.
- [ ] Inspect hoses and hydraulic cylinders for leaks or damage.
- [ ] Confirm overhead guard is intact and free of damage.

## Tires and wheels

- [ ] Inspect tires for cuts, chunking and excessive wear.
- [ ] Check pneumatic tire pressure against the rating.
- [ ] Verify wheel nuts and lugs are present and tight.
- [ ] Confirm no debris is wrapped around the axles.
- [ ] Check that tires are the correct type for the surface.
- [ ] Inspect rims for cracks or damage.

## Fluids and power

- [ ] Check engine oil, coolant and hydraulic fluid levels.
- [ ] Verify fuel or LPG level and inspect for leaks.
- [ ] On electric trucks, check battery charge, water and connections.
- [ ] Confirm the LPG cylinder is secure and the hose is sound.
- [ ] Inspect belts and the air filter where fitted.
- [ ] Check the radiator and cooling fins are clear.

## Operational checks (engine on)

- [ ] Test service and parking brakes for firm, even response.
- [ ] Check steering operates smoothly with no excess play.
- [ ] Raise, lower and tilt the mast to confirm smooth hydraulics.
- [ ] Verify the forks hold position with no hydraulic drift.
- [ ] Check gauges and warning lights read normally.
- [ ] Listen for abnormal engine or hydraulic noise.

## Safety devices

- [ ] Test the horn for clear, audible operation.
- [ ] Confirm lights, beacon and backup alarm work.
- [ ] Check the seatbelt latches and retracts. — _OSHA requires seatbelt use where the truck is equipped with one._
- [ ] Verify mirrors and visibility are clear and adjusted.
- [ ] Confirm the data plate is legible and capacity is known.
- [ ] Check the fire extinguisher where one is mounted.

## Documentation

- [ ] Record the truck number, hours and inspection date.
- [ ] Confirm the operator is trained and certified for this truck.
- [ ] Note any defects clearly with severity.
- [ ] Tag out and remove from service any truck that fails.
- [ ] Report defects to a supervisor or maintenance.
- [ ] Sign, date and file the completed daily inspection.
