# School Cleaning Checklist

A janitorial routine for cleaning classrooms, restrooms and common areas in a school.

> Free printable & editable version: [School Cleaning Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/school-cleaning-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Classrooms

- [ ] Empty all trash and replace liners.
- [ ] Disinfect desks, tables and chair surfaces.
- [ ] Wipe down high-touch points like door handles and switches.
- [ ] Clean the whiteboard or chalkboard and tray.
- [ ] Sweep and mop or vacuum the floor.
- [ ] Tidy supplies and straighten furniture.

## Restrooms

- [ ] Clean and disinfect all toilets and urinals.
- [ ] Scrub and sanitize sinks and faucet handles.
- [ ] Restock soap, paper towels and toilet paper.
- [ ] Empty trash and sanitary bins.
- [ ] Mop floors with disinfectant. — _Place wet-floor signs while cleaning._
- [ ] Wipe mirrors, partitions and door handles.

## Cafeteria

- [ ] Wipe and disinfect all tables and benches.
- [ ] Sweep and mop the dining and serving floors.
- [ ] Clean and sanitize serving and counter surfaces.
- [ ] Empty and sanitize trash and recycling bins.
- [ ] Wipe down high-touch points and seating.
- [ ] Check for spills and sticky residue under tables.

## Common areas

- [ ] Sweep and mop hallways and entryways.
- [ ] Disinfect handrails, lockers and water fountains.
- [ ] Empty trash bins in shared spaces.
- [ ] Clean entry glass and high-touch doors.
- [ ] Wipe down office and reception surfaces.
- [ ] Spot-clean walls and remove visible marks.

## Weekly deep tasks

- [ ] Scrub and machine-clean hard floors.
- [ ] Dust vents, blinds and high surfaces.
- [ ] Wash interior windows and glass.
- [ ] Deep-clean and descale restrooms.
- [ ] Disinfect shared keyboards and devices.
- [ ] Inspect and refill all supply stations.
