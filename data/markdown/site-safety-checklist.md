# Site Safety Checklist

A daily job-site safety walk covering access, PPE, equipment, hazards and housekeeping.

> Free printable & editable version: [Site Safety Checklist on ChecklistForge](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/site-safety-checklist) — tick items online or download as PDF.

## Site Access and Boundaries

- [ ] Confirm site fencing, gates and barriers are secure and intact.
- [ ] Check that authorized-personnel and warning signs are posted and legible.
- [ ] Verify pedestrian routes are separated from vehicle and plant movement. — _Mark and barricade any shared crossing points clearly._
- [ ] Confirm visitor and contractor sign-in and induction procedures are in place.
- [ ] Check access roads, ramps and walkways are firm, level and clear.
- [ ] Verify adequate lighting for early, late or enclosed work areas.

## PPE and Worker Readiness

- [ ] Confirm all personnel wear hard hats, eye protection and high-visibility clothing.
- [ ] Check that safety footwear and task-specific PPE are worn correctly.
- [ ] Verify hearing and respiratory protection is used where required. — _Match respirator and hearing protection to the actual hazard and exposure._
- [ ] Confirm workers are fit for duty and briefed on the day's tasks.
- [ ] Check that toolbox talks or pre-task briefings have been delivered and logged.

## Plant, Tools and Equipment

- [ ] Inspect powered tools and leads for damage and correct guarding.
- [ ] Confirm ladders, scaffolds and access equipment are tagged and in good condition.
- [ ] Check that mobile plant has been pre-start checked and operators are certified.
- [ ] Verify lifting equipment, chains and slings are inspected and rated for the load. — _Remove any damaged or out-of-date equipment from service immediately._
- [ ] Confirm fuel, gas and chemicals are stored and labeled correctly.

## Fall, Electrical and Excavation Hazards

- [ ] Check guardrails, covers and edge protection at all open edges and openings.
- [ ] Confirm fall protection is in place for work above the trigger height.
- [ ] Verify temporary power, leads and outlets are protected and undamaged.
- [ ] Check overhead and buried services are identified and protected.
- [ ] Confirm any excavation has protective systems and safe access in place. — _Excavations require a competent person to inspect protective systems._

## Housekeeping and Materials

- [ ] Clear walkways and work areas of debris, offcuts and trip hazards.
- [ ] Confirm materials are stacked stably and not blocking access or exits.
- [ ] Check waste and recycling are segregated and removed regularly.
- [ ] Verify spills are cleaned up and absorbents are available.
- [ ] Confirm nails, sharps and protruding rebar are removed or capped.

## Emergency and Welfare

- [ ] Confirm first aid kits, eye wash and trained first aiders are available.
- [ ] Check fire extinguishers are present, charged and unobstructed.
- [ ] Verify emergency routes, assembly points and signage are clear and current.
- [ ] Confirm emergency contact numbers and site address are posted.
- [ ] Check welfare facilities, drinking water and rest areas are clean and stocked.
- [ ] Important: this is general guidance only and does not replace OSHA regulations, formal training or your employer's safety program; confirm requirements with a qualified or competent person.
